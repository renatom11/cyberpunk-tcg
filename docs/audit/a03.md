Headline: **two "this turn" riders are mis-scoped — Reboot Optics' shield is consumed by the

(`2 xfailed`, rest of `tests/` passing).

## Findings

### AUD-reboot-optics-1 — Reboot Optics — class B4 (scope/condition: which fight)

**Printed text** (`data/cards/wnc.json`, `reboot-optics`):
> "QUICK: The next time a rival Unit fights this turn, it doesn't defeat the opposing friendly Unit."

**Leg B — clause map**

| # | Clause | Implementation | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `QUICK:` (playable as a reaction) | keyword in JSON; engine-side | ok |
| 2 | "The next time a rival Unit **fights** this turn" — the trigger is a *fight* happening | wnc.py:310 sets one `next_fight_no_defeat` mod on the controller; `steps.py:377-382` consumes it **only inside `if defeat_t:` / `if defeat_a:`** | **wrong condition** |
| 3 | "it doesn't defeat the opposing friendly Unit" — the effect | `steps.py:377-382` sets `defeat_t`/`defeat_a` to `False` for the mod holder's side | ok |
| 4 | "this turn" | `c.mod(...)` with `until_my_next_turn=False` -> `turns=0` -> dropped by `EndTurnCleanupStep` (steps.py:177) | ok |

The script turns clause 2 into an unwritten extra condition: *the next fight in which a friendly
Unit **would be defeated***. A fight that the friendly side wins, or survives for any other
reason, leaves the shield armed for the *next* fight, and the next, until the turn ends.

Two readings of "a rival Unit fights" — and both kill Psycho Squad in the scenario below:
* **broad** (a rival Unit takes part in a fight, attacking or defending): fight 1 consumes the
  shield, so fight 2 is unprotected;
* **narrow** (a rival Unit *attacks*): no rival Unit attacks on our own turn at all, so the
  shield never applies to either fight.

The script gives a third behaviour that neither reading supports. Contrast the sibling card on
the very next line, Safety Override (wnc.py:315): its trigger clause is "the next time a friendly
Unit **loses** a fight", and there consuming on the defeat is right — the two cards name
different events, and the engine treats both as "on the defeat".

**Rulings checked.** 010 (0-power / ties — not engaged: powers are 7 vs 2 and 6 vs 10),
011 and 012 (redirect scope — no BLOCKER is used; 012 is Uncertain and untouched),
013/014 (BLOCKER readiness — untouched), 023 (ATTACK triggers vs target declaration — targets are
declared explicitly), 024 (a spent attacker may be attacked later — used to set the board),
026 (field limit, Uncertain — untouched), 029 (power floor — untouched). No ruling covers when a
"the next time ... this turn" rider is used up, so this is not a D.

**Leg A — verbatim pytest output** (run before the `xfail` marker was added):

```

>       assert s.i_zone[mine] == Zone.TRASH
E       assert <Zone.FIELD: 2> == <Zone.TRASH: 4>
```

so the survival above is the Program's doing, not a mis-built board.

### AUD-cyberpsychosis-1 — Cyberpsychosis — class B2 (missing clause: tied fights)

**Printed text** (`data/cards/wnc.json`, `cyberpsychosis`):
> "QUICK: Give an equipped Unit +3 power this turn for each of its equipped Gears. If that Unit
> steals or fights, defeat it at the end of this turn."

**Leg B — clause map**

| # | Clause | Implementation | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Give an equipped Unit" — either player's, any Unit with Gear, mandatory | wnc.py:330 `c.choose([u for p in (0, 1) for u in c.units(p) if c.equipped(u)], ...)`, not optional | ok |
| 2 | "+3 power this turn for each of its equipped Gears" | wnc.py:322 `c2.temp_power(u, 3 * len(c2.gear(u)))`, cond 0, cleared at end of turn | ok |
| 3 | "If that Unit **steals**" | wnc.py:326 `ev[0] in ("steal", ...)`; `do_steal` dispatches `("steal", unit, ...)` (steps.py:311) | ok |
| 4 | "or **fights**" | wnc.py:326 `ev[0] in (..., "fight_won", "fight_lost")` | **incomplete** |
| 5 | "defeat it at the end of this turn" | wnc.py:328 `s.add_mod("defeat_at_end", u)`; `EndTurnStep` (steps.py:166-168) defeats it | ok |

Clause 4 is the hole. `fight()` (steps.py:386-389) dispatches `fight_won`/`fight_lost` **only**
when one side wins: `if a_wins: ... elif t_wins: ...`. A tie dispatches neither, so a Unit that
fought to a tie is never marked. Ruling **010** (Settled, CR 9.17-9.19) — *"Ties: both lose and
both are defeated (CR 9.17.3)"* — normally hides this, because the tied Unit is in the trash
anyway. It stops hiding it the moment anything replaces that defeat: here Deadman Transmitter
(*"If this Unit would be defeated, defeat its 'DEADMAN TRANSMITTER' instead"*, wnc.py:1104-1111),
which is also what makes the Unit "equipped" and legal for Cyberpsychosis in the first place.
The Unit fought, survived, and ends the turn alive — the printed text says it must be defeated.

The same blind spot exists in Safety Override ("the next time a friendly Unit **loses** a fight",
wnc.py:315 -> steps.py:390-394, also inside `if a_wins:`/`if t_wins:`), where under ruling 010 a
tie *is* a loss; there it has no observable consequence (see Unproven, N-NOTE 3).

**Rulings checked.** 010 (**applies** — ties: both lose and both are defeated), 016 (Gear can't be
moved — untouched), 017 (Gear can't be attacked — untouched), 029 (power floor — untouched),
030 (⊡ on Gear, Uncertain — not engaged: Deadman Transmitter is a replacement, not an activated
ability), 034 (Legends leaving play — no Legend involved), 039 (end-of-turn order: `EndTurnStep`
runs the `defeat_at_end` mods before cleanup, which the control run confirms).

**Leg A — verbatim pytest output** (run before the `xfail` marker was added):

```
        do(s, EndTurn())
>       assert s.i_zone[u] == Zone.TRASH
E       assert <Zone.FIELD: 2> == <Zone.TRASH: 4>
E        +  where <Zone.TRASH: 4> = Zone.TRASH

tests/cards/audit/test_a03.py:72: AssertionError
```

a real **loss** instead of a tie, Deadman Transmitter again taking the defeat): the Unit *is*

## Unproven

**N-NOTE 1 — Synapse Burnout counts face-up Legends once, at resolution** (wnc.py:292-293).
"A friendly Unit has +1 power for each friendly face-up Legend while fighting rival Units this
turn": `n` is frozen when the Program resolves, so Calling a Legend afterwards does not raise the
bonus. Both readings are defensible — a resolution-time pump vs. a continuous "has +1 for each"
characteristic — and the engine offers a Program no continuous mechanism at all (`power_mod`
hooks only run for cards *in play*; a Program is outside every area while it resolves and in the
trash after, ruling 035). I did **not** write a test: freezing either reading as a strict xfail
would be a coin flip. Flagged for a card-text ruling, not as a defect.

**N-NOTE 2 — Take Control's "steals 1 fewer Gig" only reaches attack-steals** (wnc.py:301).
`steal_fewer` is read in exactly one place, `ResolveAttackStep` (steps.py:334), so a steal that an
*effect* hands the same Unit is not reduced. Verified against Appetite for Destruction from this
very batch: Animals Wrecker under Take Control still stole the die its 8-margin fight win granted
(`s.gig[0] == [(6, 3)]`, `s.gig[1] == []`). Whether "steals 1 fewer Gig this turn" is a blanket
rider on that Unit or only shrinks an attack's steal count is genuinely open, so no test was
written and no expectation frozen.

**N-NOTE 3 — Safety Override shares the tied-fight blind spot, harmlessly** (wnc.py:315,
steps.py:390-394). Under ruling 010 a tie is a loss for the friendly Unit, so "the next time a
friendly Unit loses a fight this turn, defeat the opposing rival Unit" should fire; it does not.
I could not build an observable failure: on a tie the engine already sets `defeat_t` *and*
`defeat_a`, so the effect's defeat coincides with the fight's, and any replacement that saves the
opposing Unit from one would equally save it from the other. U-UNREACHABLE; recorded as a code
fact with no expectation attached.

**N-NOTE 4 — ordering in Floor It; printed-vs-current power in Detonate.**
(a) Floor It (wnc.py:277-279) queues the -1 power choice and *then* draws, so the draw resolves
before the debuff is applied; the printed order is the reverse. I looked for a printed-text
outcome that differs and found none (an empty rival field still draws; a deckout ends the game
either way), so this is style only. (b) Detonate (wnc.py:286) filters on `c.d(g).power` (printed)
rather than `c.power(g)` (current). No card in `data/cards/wnc.json` modifies a Gear's power and
every Gear has a printed power (lowest: Mandibular Upgrade 0, correctly eligible), so the two
agree today — U-UNREACHABLE for a failing test, worth knowing if a power-pumping Gear ever ships.

## Clean

Clause by clause, these eight match their printed text (rulings consulted in brackets):

* **we-gotta-live-together** (232-238) — the cost rider `3 if len(c.gigs(c.rival)) >= len(c.gigs()) + 2`
  counts *dice*, not Street Cred, and fires at exactly +2 (checked via `ops.play_cost`: +1 Gig ->
  cost 5, +2 Gigs -> cost 3); `choose_many(..., 0, 2)` is the "up to 2"; the filter is Units of
  cost <= 3 in your own trash; `play_free` pays 0. Playing two Units whose PLAY triggers both ask
  questions resolves cleanly (checked with Valentino Street Racer + Mox Inciters — both entered
  the field, both triggers ran, no crash). [034: no Legend can sit in a trash; 035: the Program
  itself can't be picked]
* **appetite-for-destruction** (241-259) — one-shot listener: `fight_won`, owner is you,
  `ev[3] >= 3` margin, winner still on the field; it removes itself before stealing; the steal is
  mandatory, from the rival, exactly one die, and runs through `push_steals`/`do_steal` so it
  emits a real `("steal", ...)` event other cards can see. Checked: a margin-1 win steals nothing,
  a margin-8 win raises "Also steal a Gig". The guide's "you don't steal when the attack was
  redirected" is overridden by the card's own text, as `docs/rules.md` instructs. [005: the stolen
  die keeps its face; 009: an empty Gig area yields nothing]
* **bootleg-black-sapphire-show** (262-271) — sells the top card (no-op on an empty deck, no
  deckout), then requires *both* parities among **your** Gigs before drawing 2 (checked: Gigs 2
  and 4 -> no draw, Eddies still +1). [036, Settled, CR 11.9.2.2 — the effect-Sell consuming the
  once-per-turn Sell action is the ruling's own default, so `EffectCtx.sell` setting `ONCE_SOLD`
  is **D**, not a finding]
* **floor-it** (275-280) — mandatory -1 on a *rival* Unit, "this turn" via `temp_power`, and the
  draw is unconditional (checked: an empty rival field still draws). [029: power floors at 0]
* **detonate** (283-286) — mandatory; rival-owned Gear only (`all_gear` walks the rival's own
  FIELD/LEGENDS lists, which is where a Gear instance lives whoever hosts it); power <= 2 (checked:
  a power-3 Adrenaline Converter offers no target at all). [017: Gear is not an attack target —
  irrelevant to an effect defeat]
* **synapse-burnout** (289-294) — friendly Unit, mandatory, `+n` gated on `FIGHTING | VS_UNIT`
  (`ops.power` requires every bit of `cond`), so it does nothing while attacking a Gig area and
  nothing against a Legend; 0 face-up Legends gives +0 (checked). See N-NOTE 1 for the snapshot
  question. [031: face-down Legends contribute nothing, which is why `faceup=True` is right]
* **take-control** (297-305) — rival Unit, mandatory, `steal_fewer` 1, expiring at the end of the
  current turn (correct whether played in your main phase or as a QUICK reaction on the rival's
  turn), and the extra draw is gated on the chosen Unit's tags (`CardDef.tags` is a `frozenset`,
  so `& {"AI", "DRONE", "VEHICLE"}` is safe; checked with Delamain Cab -> draw 1, and the -1
  reduced a power-4 Unit's single steal to none). See N-NOTE 2. [009]
* **safety-override** (313-315) — triggers on the friendly *loss*, not on the fight, which is what
  the text says; defeats the winning rival Unit; consumed exactly once; works when played as a
  QUICK reaction. See N-NOTE 3 for the tie. [010 for the tie reading]

No finding was recorded without both legs; nothing here was invented to fill a quota.
