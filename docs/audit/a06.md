### AUD-yorinobu-arasaka-steel-dragon-1 — Yorinobu Arasaka, *Steel Dragon* — class B4 (scope: friendly <-> any)

**Clause.** `data/cards/wnc.json`, `yorinobu-arasaka-steel-dragon`:
`"PLAY: You may play a Unit with cost 4 or less from your hand or trash for free. It can attack rival Units this turn.\nThe first time an ARASAKA Unit is defeated each turn, draw 1."`

**Leg B — clause map**

| # | Printed clause | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You may play a Unit ..." — optional | wnc.py:580-581 `c.choose(cands, ..., optional=True)`. Correct. |
| 2 | "with cost 4 or less" | wnc.py:578 `(c.d(i).cost or 0) <= 4`. Verified: a cost-7 Minotaur in the trash is not offered (the play returns straight to the main menu). Correct. |
| 3 | "from your hand or trash" | wnc.py:578 `c.hand() + c.trash()` (both default to `c.player`), filtered `c.is_type(i, UNIT)`. Correct. |
| 4 | "for free" | wnc.py:581 `c2.play_free(i, ...)` -> `play_card(..., cost=0)` (effects.py:288-296). Correct. |
| 5 | "It can attack rival Units this turn." | wnc.py:581 `c3.mod("attack_units_now", u)`, `turns=0` -> expires in `EndTurnCleanupStep` this turn. `legal.attack_permission` (legal.py:42) turns that into `units_ok` only, leaving `gigs_ok` False for the lagged Unit — i.e. rival **Units**, not the Gig area. Exactly the printed scope. Correct. |
| 6 | "The first time ... each turn" | wnc.py:585 `c.once("arasaka_defeated")` -> `state.use_once((inst, key))`, cleared in `EndTurnCleanupStep` (steps.py:181). Correct. |
| 7 | "an ARASAKA Unit is defeated" | wnc.py:584-585 `e[0] == "defeated" and e[2] == c.player and "ARASAKA" in c.d(e[1]).tags`. **`e[2] == c.player` is not in the text.** `ops.defeat` (ops.py:459) dispatches `("defeated", inst, owner, bool(gear))`, so `e[2]` is the defeated card's owner; the hook fires only for the controller's own ARASAKA Units. |
| 8 | "draw 1" | wnc.py:586 `c.draw(1)`. Correct. |

Why "any side" is the reading: this set qualifies a side whenever it means one. Yorinobu's own
first paragraph says "rival Units"; the nearest structural twin, `river-ward-detective-on-the-hunt`,
prints "When a **friendly** equipped Unit is defeated, ...". An unqualified "an ARASAKA Unit" next
to those is deliberate. `docs/rules.md` ("Card text beats this document") gives the text the last
word, and it says nothing about sides.

Event delivery is not the problem: `ops.dispatch` walks `act[6][s.active]`, which per
`_rebuild_active`'s docstring (ops.py:36) is "slot 4 in both delivery orders, indexed by the active
player (that player's cards first)" — both players' hooks are in it. The friendly half of the
clause works today; I ran the same board with Minotaur on Yorinobu's own field and the draw
happened. So this is scope, not a dead hook.

**Rulings checked.** 034 (a Legend leaving the field is removed from the game; DEFEATED triggers
still resolve) — not engaged, Minotaur is a Unit. 031 (face-down Legends' texts are inactive) — not
engaged, Yorinobu is a field Unit here. 019 (once-per-turn scope, Uncertain) governs *Calling*, not
`c.once`, and clause 6 is per-card-per-turn as printed. No ruling licenses the friendly restriction.
Not a D.

### AUD-placide-voodoo-sentinel-1 — Placide, *Voodoo Sentinel* — class B4 (may/must scope: an optional action gated on its conditional rider)

**Clause.** `data/cards/wnc.json`, `placide-voodoo-sentinel`:
`"PLAY / ATTACK: You may discard 1 Program. If you do, bottom-deck a rival Unit."`

**Leg B — clause map**

| # | Printed clause | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `PLAY / ATTACK` — both triggers, same effect | wnc.py:606 `CardScript(on_play=eff, on_attack=eff)`. Verified both fire (the attack path bottom-decks Psycho Squad). Correct. |
| 2 | "You may" | wnc.py:604-605 `c.maybe(yes, prompt=...)` — a yes/no. Correct in shape. |
| 3 | "discard 1 Program" — the controller's own hand, Program type only | wnc.py:600 / 602 `hand_of_type(c, PROGRAM)` (dsl.py:52-53, defaults to `c.player`), then `ops.discard`. Verified: a hand holding only a Unit offers nothing. Correct. |
| 4 | "If you do, bottom-deck a rival Unit." | wnc.py:603 `bottom_deck_one(c3, c3.rival_units())`, evaluated after the discard inside the same continuation — mandatory once the discard happened, one rival Unit, chosen by the controller. Correct. |
| 5 | *The gate* | wnc.py:604 `if progs and c.rival_units():`. The `c.rival_units()` half **is not in the text**: it makes clause 2's permission conditional on clause 4's target existing, although clause 4 hangs off "If you do". |

Both readings, stated:

* **Permissive (the one the test encodes).** The permission is to discard; "If you do" is a rider
  that fizzles when there is no rival Unit. Discarding is not a cost paid *for* the bottom-deck, and
  loading your own trash is a real line in this pool: `alt-cunningham-soulkiller-architect`
  ("1 EUR, [square]: Play a Program from your trash"), `lizzy-wizzy-delicate-weapon` ("play a Program
  with cost 3 or less from your hand **or trash** for free") and `v-streetkid` ("add 1 BRAINDANCE
  Program from your trash to your hand"). Ruling 035 exists precisely because "play a Program from
  your trash" is a real thing in this game. Placide is Blue/VOODOO BOYS, as is Lizzy Wizzy.
* **Suppressive (what the script does).** Never ask a question whose only payoff is unavailable; the
  discard is read as a cost, and a cost with no effect is not offered. This costs the engine nothing
  in any board where a rival Unit exists, and it shrinks the search tree.

I filed it because the first reading is the literal one and the option it removes is
strategically real, not null. It is the weakest of the three findings; a reviewer who reads the
discard as a cost should close it as "working as intended" rather than change the script.

**Rulings checked.** 035 (a Program resolving is outside every area) — cited above as evidence that
trash-Programs matter; does not settle this. 020 (no hand-size limit) — no pressure to discard from
that direction. 022 (bottom-deck semantics) — clause 4 uses `ops.bottom_deck`, correct. Nothing in
the 41 licenses the gate. Not a D.

## Unproven

**U-1 — S-SUSPICION — Yorinobu Arasaka *Steel Dragon* does not draw for its own defeat.**
Yorinobu is itself an ARASAKA Unit, so "The first time an ARASAKA Unit is defeated each turn, draw 1"
arguably covers its own death. `ops.defeat` (ops.py:456-461) calls `move(...)` *before*
`dispatch(("defeated", ...))`, so by dispatch time Yorinobu is in the trash, out of
`_rebuild_active`'s per-player lists, and its `on_event` hook is not called. I wrote the scenario
and it behaved as the engine describes; I did **not** file it, because this is a whole-engine
convention (a continuous "when X happens" ability on a card that has just left play stops applying),
not something this card's script chose, and changing it would touch every `on_event` card in the
pool. The printed text does not distinguish. Flag for a rules-level decision, not a card fix. Once
AUD-yorinobu-arasaka-steel-dragon-1 is fixed, this becomes the only remaining gap in clause 7.

**U-2 — S-SUSPICION — Off-Duty Malfini can "spend" an already-spent rival Unit.**
`spend_one(c, c.rival_units())` (wnc.py:522-524) offers every rival Unit, spent ones included, and
`ops.spend` (ops.py:320-322) returns immediately if `i_spent` is set — so a player can throw the
clause away. I wrote the board (one ready + one spent rival Unit) and the engine did offer both
picks. It is not a finding: "Spend this Unit and a rival Unit" places no readiness condition on the
target, and choosing a redundant target is the player's prerogative in every comparable card. The
mandatory half — Malfini spends itself even when the rival's field is empty — behaves correctly.

## Clean

Ten cards read clause by clause against the text, `docs/rules.md` and all 41 rulings, and found
correct. Each line names the check that could have gone wrong and the probe that says it did not.

* **maxtac-av** — "PLAY: You may swap a friendly Gig with a rival Gig." (wnc.py:499-505) `optional=True`
  on the friendly pick carries the "may"; the inner `choose_gig([c2.rival], ...)` is correctly
  mandatory once you have opted in; `swap_gig(i, j)` -> `ops.swap_gigs(s, self.player, mine, theirs)`
  keeps the sides straight, and the index `i` closed over is an `int`, not a stale `ctx`, so the
  dsl.py "use the ctx you are given" rule is not broken. Probed: declining leaves both Gig areas
  untouched, and a rival with no Gigs produces no swap. Ruling 005 (stolen dice keep their face) is
  about stealing, not swapping; 037 (off-face adjustment fails) does not apply — a swap moves whole
  dice, not faces.
* **minotaur** — "PLAY: If you have more [star] than a Rival, defeat a rival Unit with power 5 or
  less." (wnc.py:508-510) `c.more_cred()` is strict `>`; the defeat is mandatory as printed;
  `power_le(c, 5)` uses live `ops.power`. Probed: equal Street Cred defeats nothing. Ruling 038 (Null
  Street Cred) is reached only by a player with zero Gigs, which `more_cred` treats as 0 — and a Gig
  area with dice can never sum to 0, so the comparison is right on every reachable board. 029 (power
  floors at 0) makes a 0-power Unit a legal <=5 target, which it is here.
* **offduty-malfini** — "PLAY: Spend this Unit and a rival Unit." (wnc.py:519-524) Spends itself
  first, unconditionally, then a rival Unit; both halves mandatory. Probed: with an empty rival
  field it still spends itself. See U-2.
* **pacifica-netrunner** — "PLAY: If your [star] is an even number, a rival Unit can't ready until
  your next turn." (wnc.py:527-532) `c.cred_even()` implements ruling 038 exactly (effects.py:95-100:
  with no Gigs, Null — neither even nor odd — returns False); probed, a Gig-less controller flags
  nothing. `cant_ready` sets `F_NO_READY_NEXT`, which `ReadyStep` (steps.py:114-115) consumes at the
  target's *own* next Ready step, i.e. the rival's next turn — the one ready the clause denies.
* **royce-dont-call-me-simon** — "PLAY: Defeat a rival Unit with power 2 or less. If you have more
  [star] than a Rival, defeat a rival Unit with power 3 or less instead." (wnc.py:535-537) The
  "instead" is a threshold swap, not a second defeat, and the script is a single `defeat_one` with
  `3 if c.more_cred() else 2`. Probed both branches: without more Street Cred a power-3 Unit
  survives; with it, it dies.
* **tygers-whisper** — "PLAY: You may Call a Legend for free. (You can only Call a Legend once per
  turn.)" (wnc.py:540-542) `c.offer_call_free()` -> `ops.can_call_free` checks `ONCE_CALLED` and the
  existence of a face-down Legend, and `ops.call_legend` sets `ONCE_CALLED` — ruling **041** ("Call a
  Legend may be used only once per turn even if a game action tells you to", CR 5.7.3) and ruling
  019 (per-turn reset scope, Uncertain — the script defers to the engine's counter and does not
  deviate from the ruling's default, so **D** on the scope question). The choice is blind, per the
  guide's "without looking first"; `optional=True` carries the "may". Probed: all Legends face-up ->
  no offer. The second reminder line ("Units with power 0 don't steal Gigs") is engine-side in
  `ops.steal_count` and correctly absent from the script.
* **valentino-street-racer** — "PLAY: Give another friendly Unit with cost 5 or less ADRENALINE this
  turn." (wnc.py:545-548) `c.units()` is friendly-only, `u != c.inst` is "another", `cost <= 5` reads
  the printed cost. `c.grant` adds a `kw` mod with `turns=0`, which `EndTurnCleanupStep`
  (steps.py:180) drops at the end of this turn — probed across a full turn cycle: the keyword is
  gone. Probed: a cost-7 Unit is not a legal target.
* **viktor-vektor-you-might-feel-a-little-pinch** — "PLAY: Play a CYBERWARE Gear with cost 2 or less
  from your trash for free. Equip it only to another friendly Unit." (wnc.py:551-562) All four
  filters present (`is_type GEAR`, `"CYBERWARE" in tags`, `cost <= 2`, `c.trash()`), `cost=0` for
  free, and the host list is `[u for u in c2.units() if u != c2.inst]` — friendly, a Unit, and not
  Viktor. Probed: a cost-4 CYBERWARE Gear in the trash is not offered, and with only a face-up
  Legend available as a host the Gear stays in the trash (`legal.gear_hosts`, which would allow a
  Legend, is correctly *not* used here — the card says "Unit").
* **sandayu-oda-hanakos-guardian** — "PLAY: Spend a rival Unit for each friendly value-pair of Gigs.
  / This Unit can attack rival Units the turn it's played." (wnc.py:565-571) `c.value_pairs()`
  defaults to the controller ("friendly") and counts each die in at most one pair; `choose_many(n, n)`
  spends exactly that many, clamped to what exists. Probed: two pairs -> exactly 2 rival Units spent;
  zero pairs -> none. The second clause is `attack_perm=lambda c, b: (True, b[1]) if c.s.i_lag[c.inst]
  else b` — it returns `gigs_ok` untouched, so a just-played Sandayu may attack a rival **Unit** and
  not the Gig area, which is what the text grants. Probed: with a spent rival Unit and a rival Gig
  area holding a die, declaring the attack produced no target question at all, i.e. the Unit was the
  only legal target. `legal.attack_permission` consults `attack_perm` *after* both `cant_attack`
  early-returns and after MaxTac Suppression Team's unconditional veto (legal.py:28-43), so this
  grant cannot override a prohibition. Ruling 039 (Lag is cleared from all Units at the end of every
  turn) is what makes `i_lag` a faithful stand-in for "the turn it's played".
* **dexter-deshawn-one-last-chance** — "PLAY / ATTACK: Adjust a Gig by up to 1. / DEFEATED: If your
  [star] differs from a Rival's by 10+, draw 2." (wnc.py:602-609) Both timing triggers wired
  (`on_play`, `on_attack`) plus `on_defeated`. `adjust_up_to([c.player, c.rival], -1, 1)` covers
  either player's Gig — "a Gig" is unqualified, and this matches every other unqualified
  "Adjust a Gig" script in the set (`afterparty-at-lizzies` wnc.py:343, `zetatech-faceplate`
  wnc.py:1052, `muamar-reyes-el-capitan` wnc.py:1180). "Up to 1" is +/-1 plus a decline, and
  `ops.adjust_gig` enforces ruling **037** (an adjustment off the die's face fails) — **D** on the
  clamping question. The DEFEATED half is `abs(c.cred() - c.cred(c.rival)) >= 10` -> `draw(2)`:
  "differs by 10+" is symmetric and inclusive, as written. Probed: a difference of 6 draws nothing.
