### AUD-el-sombreron-la-venganza-lenta-1 — El Sombrerón, *La Venganza Lenta* — **B1 wrong effect**

**Clause at issue:** "…this Unit gains power equal to a friendly **max Gig** this turn."

**Leg B — clause map**

Printed text: *"ATTACK: You may pay 2 €$. If you do, this Unit gains power equal to a friendly max
Gig this turn."*

| # | Clause | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `ATTACK:` trigger | `CardScript(on_attack=attack)` — wnc.py:677. ✔ |
| 2 | "You may pay 2 €$" | `c.maybe(yes, prompt=...)` wnc.py:676 + `pay(c2.s, c2.player, 2)` wnc.py:674; gated by `available(c.s, c.player) < 2` (wnc.py:670) so the option only appears when 2 €$ can actually be paid. ✔ |
| 3 | "If you do" | the payment and the power gain are both inside `yes`, so no pay ⇒ no power. ✔ |
| 4 | "this Unit" | `c2.temp_power(c2.inst, …)` — the source itself. ✔ |
| 5 | "gains power equal to a friendly **max Gig**" | **wnc.py:675 `max(c2.gig_values())` — WRONG.** That is the largest *value* among friendly Gigs. A "max Gig" is a die showing its maximum face. |
| 6 | "friendly" | `c2.gig_values()` defaults to `c.player`. ✔ |
| 7 | "this turn" | `temp_power` with `cond=0`; `EndTurnCleanupStep` clears `s.temp_power` (steps.py:179). ✔ |

Why clause 5 is wrong, from the text field alone (authority 1):

* Seven cards in `wnc.json` use this vocabulary. Six say **"min Gig"** — `alt-cunningham-soulkiller-architect`,
  `chrome-reverie`, `jackie-welles-pour-one-out-for-me`, `pyramid-song`, `three-mouths-one-desire`,
  `trust-no-one`. El Sombrerón is the only card that says **"max Gig"**.
* `EffectCtx.min_gigs` (effects.py:120-122) is documented *"Indices of dice showing their minimum
  face (1)"* and is what all six "min Gig" cards are scripted with (wnc.py:111, 179, 361, 1320, 1331).
  So "min Gig" is not "the lowest die in the area" — it is "a die on its floor".
* The mirror term must mean "a die on its ceiling". `EffectCtx.max_gigs` (effects.py:124) implements
  exactly that (`v == k`) and is the **only Gig helper in `effects.py` that no script anywhere in the
  repo calls** — it was written for this card and never wired up.
* Consequence: with Gigs `d12=9, d4=4` the only max Gig is the d4, so the gain is **+4**, not +9. The
  two readings also differ in who picks ("a friendly max Gig" lets the player choose among several);
  the test board has exactly one max Gig so the choice question does not confound it.

**Rulings checked:** 037 (adjusting a Gig off-face — governs the *values* a die may show, confirms a
die is a (faces, value) pair and that `v == k` is a meaningful, reachable state); 025
(payment order — Approximation; `pay` uses the auto-pay order, which is the ruling's default, so **not**
a deviation); 038 (Null Street Cred — not engaged, this clause reads a die, not Cred); 005 (stolen dice
keep their face value — so a stolen die can legitimately be a max Gig). **None of them covers "max Gig";
this is a plain reading of the card text.**

### AUD-goro-takemura-losing-his-way-1 — Goro Takemura, *Losing His Way* — **A genuine ambiguity** (would be B4 under the vacuous-truth reading)

**Clause at issue:** "If **all** friendly Legends are face-up…" — what happens with **zero** friendly
Legends.

**Leg B — clause map**

Printed text: *"ATTACK: If all friendly Legends are face-up, this Unit has +5 power this turn."*

| # | Clause | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `ATTACK:` trigger | `CardScript(on_attack=…)` wnc.py:682. ✔ |
| 2 | "all friendly Legends" | `c.legends()` — the owner's `LEGENDS` zone. Correctly excludes a Legend that has GONE SOLO (it sits in `FIELD`, i.e. not "in your Legends area"). ✔ |
| 3 | "are face-up" | `all(c.s.i_faceup[l] for l in c.legends())`. ✔ |
| 4 | **the empty case** | `c.legends() and …` (wnc.py:683) adds an unwritten clause: *"…and you control at least one Legend"*. **Disputed.** |
| 5 | "this Unit has +5 power" | `c.temp_power(c.inst, 5)`. ✔ |
| 6 | "this turn" | `cond=0`; cleared by `EndTurnCleanupStep` (steps.py:179). ✔ |

**Reading 1 (asserted by the test): vacuously true.** A universal over an empty set is true, the
convention every major TCG uses. The text names no existence requirement; when this set wants a
count it says so (Panam Palmer: "draw 1 **for each** friendly face-up Legend" — 0 Legends ⇒ 0 cards,
no special case). Under this reading Goro attacks at 4+5 = 9 with an empty Legends area; the script
gives 4.

**Reading 2 (what the script does): the condition presupposes at least one Legend.** "If all friendly
Legends are face-up" can be read as "if your Legends are (all) face-up", i.e. as a statement about
Legends you have; and the card's design intent is plainly to reward Calling your Legends, not to
reward having none. Under this reading wnc.py:683 is correct as written.

Both are defensible from the text alone, so this is filed as **A**, not B — but the test is written
for Reading 1 and marked `xfail(strict=True)`, so whichever way the rules end up falling the repo
now has an executable record of the difference.

**Reachability.** An empty Legends area is reachable: **ruling 015** (`go_solo_vacates_slot` = true,
Uncertain) — *"its slot sits empty, and when it leaves the field it is removed from the game"* — so
Go-Soloing all three Legends empties the area, as does **ruling 034** (`legends_removed_when_leaving`,
Settled (CR 4.4)) for any Legend sent out of the Legends area. The finding **depends on** ruling 015's
default rather than deviating from it, so the presumed-D trap on "GO SOLO slot vacation" does not
apply — nothing here contests what happens to the slot, only how Goro reads an area that ruling 015
says can be empty. The test builds the empty area directly with `Side(legends=[])`, so it does not
re-litigate the GO SOLO path.

**Rulings checked:** 015 (slot vacation — Uncertain; relied on, not contradicted); 034 (Legends
removed when they leave — Settled); 031 (face-down Legends' texts inactive — not engaged: this clause
reads the *orientation* of a Legend, not its text); 003 (a Legend spent as an Eddie stays in the
Legends area, so spending Legends can never empty the area); 027 (a spent Legend may GO SOLO — widens
reachability). No ruling addresses a vacuous universal.

## Unproven

### U-1 · `swordwise-huscle` · "If this Unit has power 5+" is measured with `sit=ATTACKING` only — **S-SUSPICION**

wnc.py:632 evaluates `c.power(sit=ATTACKING)`, so a power bonus that is conditional on `FIGHTING`
or `VS_UNIT` (e.g. Synapse Burnout, wnc.py:293, `temp_power_one(..., FIGHTING | VS_UNIT)`) does not
count toward the 5 even when the declared target is a rival Unit and the fight is one step away.
I wrote the straightforward probes and they **passed**: at printed 3 + Mantis Blades 2 the unit is
power 5 in every situation and draws (`power(s, u) == power(s, u, ATTACKING) == 5`). The only board
that separates the readings needs a `FIGHTING`-conditional buff, and on that board the plain reading
of "has power 5+" at trigger time (ruling 023: the trigger resolves after the target is declared but
before the fight) is that a fight-only bonus is *not* yet part of the Unit's power — which is what
the script does. Recorded as suspicion only; no finding.

### U-2 · `pepe-najarro-working-doubles` · MERC tags are read off face-down Legends — **S-SUSPICION**

wnc.py:653 filters `c.legends()` by `"MERC" in c.d(l).tags` without regard to `i_faceup`, so a
face-down spent MERC Legend is a legal pick. I wrote the probe and it **passed** under the reading I
believe correct: the text says "MERC Legends **in your Legends area**", with no face-up requirement,
and **ruling 031** withholds only a face-down Legend's *text* ("only face-up Legends contribute
static effects, event triggers and abilities"), not its identity or tags — which its controller
knows anyway. Contrast Panam and Synapse Burnout, which say "face-up" when they mean it. No finding.

* **`evelyn-parker-scheming-siren`** (wnc.py:616-622) — "ATTACK: Draw 1. Then, if you have more ★

  `EffectCtx.discard` clamps to hand size so an empty hand is a no-op rather than a crash. Ruling 038

  gives ≥ 1 and "no Gigs vs some Gigs" already compares as Null < n; probed both the less-Cred board
  (draw, no discard) and the no-Gigs board (no discard). The power-0 reminder is engine-side
  (`steal_count(0) == 0`).
* **`swordwise-huscle`** (wnc.py:630-632) — "ATTACK: If this Unit has power 5+, draw 1." `>= 5` for
  "5+"; power includes Gear (`ops.power` sums the host's Gear) and temporary mods; exactly-5 draws.
  See U-1 for the one unresolved nuance.
* **`panam-palmer-strength-through-family`** (wnc.py:635-646) — two paragraphs, both present.
  "During your turn, you may Call a Legend for free": `Ability(cost=0, legal=is_active_turn and
  can_call_free)` with `self_spend=False`, so it neither spends Panam nor is blocked by Lag — correct,

  action tells you to") and ruling 019's `once_per_turn_scope = "turn"` default. `offer_call_free`

  ready up to 2 MERC Legends in your Legends area." `has_value_pair()` is friendly-only and counts

* **`jackie-welles-ride-or-die-choom`** (wnc.py:658-662) — both triggers. ATTACK: `2 * (# friendly Gigs
  with even value)` via `temp_power(c.inst, …)`; DEFEATED: `draw(# friendly Gigs with odd value)`.
  `gig_values()` defaults to the owner, i.e. friendly; even/odd are `v % 2`, and ruling 038's Null Cred

  attack, gone on the next turn (`EndTurnCleanupStep` clears `temp_power`). Ruling 023's ordering means
  the ATTACK bonus is live for the steal that follows, and a die stolen during that attack is in the
  area for a later DEFEATED count — both consistent with the text.
* **`screw-lovelorn-fool`** (wnc.py:686-690) — "DEFEATED: Add another Unit from your trash to your
  hand." Mandatory (`optional` not set), own trash, `is_type(i, UNIT)` (Gear and Programs excluded),
  and `i != c.inst` implements "**another**" — Screw is already in the trash when the trigger resolves
  (`ops.defeat` moves, then pushes the trigger), so without that guard he could return himself. A
  second copy of Screw *is* a legal pick, as "another" should allow. Probed: with no Unit in the trash
  nothing is asked and nothing moves. Ruling 034 (Legends leave to REMOVED) means no Legend can ever be

  all friendly face-down Legends. Then, you may Call a Legend for free." `look_at` marks every friendly
  face-down Legend known to its controller and emits the `look` event; the Call is offered afterwards,
