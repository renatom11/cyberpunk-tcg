### AUD-kiroshi-optics-1 — equip restriction: "a Unit", not "a friendly Unit"

- **Card**: `kiroshi-optics` (Yellow Gear, RAM 1, cost 1, power 1)
- **Class**: **B4** (scope: friendly ↔ rival). Interacts with **C**: the card text contradicts
  `docs/rules.md` ("Gear — pay its cost and equip it to a friendly Unit or Legend"), and by the
  guide's own words card text wins.
- **Clause**: `(Equip to a Unit or friendly face-up Legend.)`

**Leg B — clause map**

Printed text (`data/cards/wnc.json`):

```
(Equip to a Unit or friendly face-up Legend.)
ATTACK: Look at a friendly face-down Legend. (Don't reveal it.)
```

| # | Clause | Implementation | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "(Equip to **a Unit** …" | nothing in wnc.py 715-718; hosts come from `legal.gear_hosts` (`src/cptcg/core/legal.py:18-20`) which returns `s.units(player)` — **friendly Units only** | **unimplemented** |
| 2 | "… or friendly face-up Legend.)" | `legal.gear_hosts` adds `[i for i in s.legends(player) if s.i_faceup[i]]` | correct |
| 3 | "ATTACK: Look at a friendly face-down Legend." | wnc.py 716-718: `on_attack=lambda c: c.choose(c.legends(faceup=False), lambda c2, l: c2.look_at(l), …)`; `legends()` defaults to `c.player` = the Gear's owner (`effects.py:55-59`), so "friendly" is right, and the choice is non-optional, so "Look at" is mandatory | correct |
| 4 | "(Don't reveal it.)" | `look_at` sets only `i_known[inst] \|= 1 << self.player` (`effects.py:382-384`) — the rival learns nothing | correct |

Why clause 1 is a real restriction and not sloppy wording: of the 17 Gear in MS01-WNC, **9** print
`(Equip to a friendly Unit or face-up Legend.)`, **7** print no parenthetical at all, and exactly
**one** — Kiroshi Optics — prints `(Equip to a Unit or friendly face-up Legend.)`. The word
"friendly" moved, deliberately: it now qualifies only the Legend. A 1-cost 1-power CYBERWARE eye
that can be bolted onto a rival's Unit (its ATTACK trigger still looks at *your* face-down Legend,
since `c.player` is the Gear's owner) is a coherent design; the engine simply has no per-card host
hook — `gear_hosts` is called unconditionally from `legal.py:142` and from
`EffectCtx.play_free` (`effects.py:288-295`).

**Rulings checked**: 016 (Gear can't be re-equipped after play — not about *who* may host),
017 (Gear can't be attacked — irrelevant), 031 (face-down Legends' texts are inactive —
consistent with the "face-up Legend" half of the clause), 034 (a Legend leaving play is removed;
its Gear follows). **None of the 41 rulings settles who may host Gear**, so this is not a
presumed-D case: the trap list covers BLOCKER redirect scope, GO SOLO slot vacation, once-per-turn
scope, field size, squares-on-Gear and payment order — not Gear host legality.

### AUD-dying-night-vs-pistol-1 — "if this Unit is named V" also fires for a Legend host

- **Card**: `dying-night-vs-pistol` (Blue Gear, RAM 2, cost 2, power 2)
- **Class**: **B4** (scope: Unit ↔ Legend; the condition is looser than printed)
- **Clause**: `At the end of your turn, if this Unit is named "V", ready 2 Eddies.`

**Leg B — clause map**

Printed text:

```
ATTACK: Decrease a Gig by up to 2. At the end of your turn, if this Unit is named "V", ready 2 Eddies.
```

| # | Clause | Implementation (wnc.py 705-714) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "ATTACK: Decrease a Gig by up to 2." | `on_attack=lambda c: c.adjust_up_to([c.player, c.rival], -2, -1, prompt="Decrease a Gig")` — both Gig areas, amounts −2/−1, plus a decline option for the "up to" | correct (see below) |
| 2 | "At the end of your turn" | `ev`: `e[0] == "end_turn" and e[1] == c.player`, with `events=frozenset({"end_turn"})`; `c.player` is the Gear's owner, so "your" is the Gear controller's turn | correct |
| 3 | "if **this Unit** is named \"V\"" | `h = c.host(); … h >= 0 and c.d(h).name == "V"` — checks the host's **name** only, never that the host is a Unit | **wrong scope** |
| 4 | "ready 2 Eddies" | `c.ready_eddies(2)` → `ops.ready_eddies(s, c.player, 2)` | correct |

Clause 1 detail: "a Gig" unqualified means either player's Gig in this set's vocabulary —
`jackie-welles-pour-one-out-for-me` says "decrease **a friendly Gig** by up to 2" and
`trust-no-one` says "Decrease a Gig by up to 3" and is scripted `[c.player, c.rival]`
(wnc.py 357-364). `adjust_up_to` with `lo=-2, hi=-1` also filters to amounts that land on a real
face (`effects.py:355-375`), per **ruling 037** ("Adjusting a Gig to a value not on its face …
the effect fails"; "up to N" effects let you pick a legal amount). A control test in the audit
file confirms 5 options (2 own + 2 rival + decline) on a `(8,5)` / `(6,3)` board and passes.

Clause 3 is the defect. A Gear's legal hosts include friendly face-up Legends, and two of the
three cards named "V" in this set are Legends — `v-streetkid` and `v-corporate-exile` — against
one Unit, `v-roamer-of-the-badlands`. Equip Dying Night to a face-up `V: Streetkid` sitting in the
Legends area and the script readies 2 Eddies every turn, even though a Legend in the Legends area
is not a Unit.

Why "this Unit" is load-bearing rather than loose wording: this set distinguishes the two
explicitly whenever it means both. `zetatech-faceplate` — the other Gear with a non-trigger
ongoing clause — reads "When **this Unit or Legend** is spent, …", and all 9 standard equip
parentheticals read "a friendly **Unit or face-up Legend**". Dying Night says "this Unit". A
face-up Legend that has **GO SOLO**'d onto the field *is* a Unit (ruling 015: "it is a Unit now"),
and that case should still pay out — so the correct discriminator is the host's **zone**
(`Zone.FIELD` = Unit, `Zone.LEGENDS` = Legend), not its card type.

**Alternative reading** (why this is a finding and not class A): one could read "this Unit" as
shorthand for "the card this Gear is equipped to". But then `zetatech-faceplate`'s "this Unit or
Legend" would be redundant, and the equip parentheticals would not need to name Legends
separately. The set's own vocabulary decides it.

**Rulings checked**: 015 (GO SOLO Legend on the field is a Unit — **applies**, and is the reason
the fix must be zone-based, not `CardType`-based; it is Uncertain only about slot vacation and
spendability, not about it being a Unit), 027 (a spent Legend may GO SOLO), 031 (face-down Legends
are inert — so only a face-up Legend can ever host this and reach the bug), 034 (Legend leaving
play is removed), 040 (face-up Legend spendability). No ruling licenses reading "this Unit" as
"this Unit or Legend".

### AUD-the-relic-experimental-biochip-1 — "Then, bottom-deck this Unit" is skipped when the trash has no Unit to recur

- **Card**: `the-relic-experimental-biochip` (Yellow Gear, RAM 4, cost 5, power 3)
- **Class**: **B4** (condition error — the second sentence is gated on the first having found a
  target). Shades into **B2** (the clause goes entirely unperformed in that branch).
- **Clause**: `Then, bottom-deck this Unit.`

**Leg B — clause map**

Printed text:

```
(Equip to a friendly Unit or face-up Legend.)
DEFEATED: Play another Unit with cost 9 or less from your trash for free. Then, bottom-deck this Unit.
```

| # | Clause | Implementation (wnc.py 720-732) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "(Equip to a friendly Unit or face-up Legend.)" | `legal.gear_hosts` = friendly Units + friendly face-up Legends — an exact match | correct |
| 2 | "DEFEATED:" (Gear fires when its **host** is defeated) | `ops.defeat` (`src/cptcg/core/ops.py:452-458`) stamps `add_mod("was_host", g, inst)` and pushes `Trigger.DEFEATED` for each Gear before the host's own trigger | correct |
| 3 | "Play **another** Unit …" | `cands = [… i != host]`, with `host` = last `mod_values("was_host", c.inst)` | correct (control test passes) |
| 4 | "… with cost 9 or less …" | `(c.d(i).cost or 0) <= 9` | correct |
| 5 | "… from **your** trash …" | `c.trash()` defaults to `c.player` = the Gear's owner | correct |
| 6 | "… for free." | `c2.play_free(i)` → `play_card(…, cost=0)` | correct |
| 7 | "Then, bottom-deck **this Unit**." | inside the `after` continuation only: `if host is not None and c2.s.i_zone[host] is Zone.TRASH: c2.bottom_deck(host)` | **skipped whenever clause 3-5 yields no candidate**, because `c.choose([], after)` with no `otherwise=` returns without running anything (`effects.py:156-160`) |

So a Relic host that dies with an empty (or Unit-less) trash stays in the trash instead of going to
the bottom of the deck — the card's whole recursion-denial half silently does nothing. The gap is
entirely in the empty-candidate branch: when there *is* a Unit to recur, clause 7 fires correctly
(second control test passes).

Why the printed text says clause 7 still happens: this set uses "Then," as a **sequencing** word,
not a dependency. The immediately preceding card in the same wnc.py section,
`t-bug-amateur-philosopher` — "DEFEATED: Look at all friendly face-down Legends. Then, you may
Call a Legend for free." — is scripted (wnc.py 695-700) to call `c.offer_call_free()`
unconditionally, including when there are zero face-down Legends to look at.
`evelyn-parker-scheming-siren` ("Draw 1. Then, if you have more ★ …, discard 1.") and
`yorinobu-arasaka-embracing-destruction` are the same shape. An instruction that cannot be carried
out is skipped; the rest of the effect still resolves.

**Alternative reading**: `trust-no-one` is scripted the other way — its "Then, if you control a min
Gig, draw 1" hangs off `adjust_up_to(..., cont=after)`, which does not run on decline. So the repo
is internally inconsistent about "Then,". A reader who takes "Then," as strictly contingent on the
first sentence having been performed would call the current Relic behaviour correct. I judge the
sequencing reading better (it is what the neighbouring DEFEATED Gear/Legend cards do, and it is the
only reading under which a mandatory instruction with no legal target does not erase the rest of
its own sentence), but the disagreement is recorded here rather than hidden.

**Rulings checked**: 022 (deck order / bottom-deck semantics — the destination is right),
034 (**applies, and the script handles it**: with a face-up Legend host, ruling 034 removes the
Legend from the game, so the `i_zone[host] is Zone.TRASH` guard correctly declines to bottom-deck
it — and "this **Unit**" would not name a Legend anyway; this is a **D** sub-case, not a finding),
035 (Programs resolve outside every area — not applicable, the recurred card is a Unit),
026 (field size Uncertain, `field_limit = null`, so a full field never blocks the recursion —
presumed D and untouched). Nothing in the 41 rulings addresses "Then,".

## Unproven

- **N-NOTE — `gear_hosts` excludes face-down Legends, engine-wide.** `docs/rules.md` says
  "Gear — pay its cost and equip it to a friendly Unit **or Legend**" with no face-up requirement,
  and `dying-night-vs-pistol` (like 6 other Gear) prints **no** equip parenthetical at all, so its
  hosts come straight from that sentence. `legal.gear_hosts` nonetheless requires
  `s.i_faceup[i]`. This is a coherent engine-wide policy (ruling 031: a face-down Legend's text is
  inactive, and its identity is hidden, so hosting one leaks nothing but is odd), it is shared by
  every Gear in the game, and 9 of 17 Gear print "face-up Legend" explicitly — so it is a
  cross-cutting rules-doc/engine question, not a defect in the three scripts in this range.
  Recorded as style/scope note only; no test written.
- **S-SUSPICION — The Relic on a face-up Legend host.** I reasoned through (but did not need a
  failing test for) the case where the host is a face-up Legend: ruling 034 removes the Legend from
  the game and the Gear follows, so `i_zone[host] is Zone.TRASH` is false and nothing is
  bottom-decked. That is the ruling's own default, and "this Unit" does not name a Legend. Correct
  as scripted — **D** by ruling 034, no finding.

## Clean

- **`kiroshi-optics`** — the ATTACK half is exact: mandatory "Look at" over the Gear owner's own
  face-down Legends (`legends(faceup=False)` defaults to `c.player`), with `look_at` setting only
  the looker's knowledge bit, so "(Don't reveal it.)" holds. Verified by a passing control test
  that also asserts the rival's knowledge bit stays clear. Only the equip clause is wrong
  (AUD-kiroshi-optics-1).
- **`dying-night-vs-pistol`** — "ATTACK: Decrease a Gig by up to 2" is exact: both Gig areas
  ("a Gig" is unqualified in a set that writes "a friendly Gig" when it means friendly), amounts
  −1/−2 only, a decline option for "up to", and off-face amounts filtered per ruling 037. The
  end-of-turn timing, the "your turn" scope and `ready_eddies(2)` are all correct; only the
  Unit-vs-Legend host test is loose (AUD-dying-night-vs-pistol-1).
- **`the-relic-experimental-biochip`** — equip restriction, DEFEATED-fires-for-the-host plumbing,
  "another" (host excluded), "cost 9 or less", "your trash", "for free", and the bottom-deck itself
  when a Unit *is* recurred are all correct; the Legend-host case is right by ruling 034. Only the
  no-candidate branch drops clause 7 (AUD-the-relic-experimental-biochip-1).
