## Findings

### AUD-misty-olszewski-mender-of-broken-spirits-1 — class **B2** (missing clause)

**Card:** `misty-olszewski-mender-of-broken-spirits` (Misty Olszewski, *Mender of Broken Spirits*),
MS01-WNC #119, Blue Unit, RAM 2, cost 3, power 0, MYSTIC, `verified: true`.

**Printed text** (`data/cards/wnc.json`):

> This Unit can't attack.
> At the end of your turn, choose a card type. Then, **reveal** the top card of your deck. If it's
> the chosen type, add it to your hand and ready 1 Eddie. Otherwise, trash it. (Unit, Gear, and
> Program are card types.)

**The clause:** "Then, **reveal** the top card of your deck."

**Why clause 4 is a real clause and not flavour.** This set uses the two words as opposites: Kiroshi
Optics prints "ATTACK: **Look at** a friendly face-down Legend. *(Don't reveal it.)*" — so inside
the printed card pool, "reveal" means the thing "look at" is explicitly not. `docs/rules.md` uses
"reveal" the same way for Sell ("sell a sell-tagged card from hand: **reveal it**", line 181), and
`ruling 002` is built on that reveal transferring information to the Rival:

> | 002 | Are the identities of cards in an Eddies area public? | **Yes, as an unordered multiset.**
> Selling requires revealing the card; **both players saw it.** … | `perfect_eddie_memory` = true | Settled |

**Where the gap is observable.** `core.view.knows_identity` (view.py:140-141) answers the HAND case
with `me is None or s.i_owner[inst] == me or inst in _pinned(s, me)`, and `_pinned` reads only
`s.pending.revealed` — which is empty here and, being on the pending Choice, transient anyway. So:

* mismatch branch → the card lands in `Zone.TRASH`, a `PUBLIC_ZONE` (view.py:111): the Rival sees
  it, and the reveal is satisfied **by accident**;
* match branch → the card goes `DECK -> HAND` and the Rival never learns it. That is the failure.

**Rulings checked.** 002 (quoted above — the reveal-is-public precedent, applies); 022 (deck order,
index −1 is the top — confirms `top(1)` reads the card the card names, applies, satisfied); 031
(face-down Legend texts inactive — the only other consumer of `i_known`, does not apply); 018
(hidden mulligan — the one place the repo deliberately hides a decision, not a reveal, does not
apply); 035 (Programs resolve outside every area — does not apply). No ruling makes a printed
reveal private, and none of the five "presumed D" trap areas (BLOCKER redirect scope, GO SOLO slot
vacation, once-per-turn scope, field size, squares-on-Gear, payment order) is touched.

Not **D**: no ruling covers it. Not **A**: "reveal" has one meaning, and the set's own Kiroshi
Optics reminder pins it. Not **C**: `docs/rules.md` agrees with the card. Classed **B2** rather than
B3/B4 because the clause is simply absent, not mis-scoped.

**Related, not duplicate.** A02 filed `AUD-fool-on-the-hill-1` for the same printed word, but there
the un-revealed cards are shown to the player who must *decide* (fixable in the script with
`revealed=`). Here the audience is the Rival, who makes no decision, and `revealed=` cannot express
that: the engine has no durable "reveal to the opponent" for a non-Legend card. Eight cards in the
pool print "reveal"; this one is Misty's instance of it.

## Unproven

**U-1 · N-NOTE — "reveal" is a pool-wide engine gap, not eight card bugs.**
`i_known` is only ever written by `ops.call_legend` (ops.py:547) and `effects.look_at`
(effects.py:383), and `view.knows_identity` reads it only for the LEGENDS zone; `Choice.revealed`
pins instances for the *chooser* and only while the Choice is pending. So there is no mechanism a
card script could call to reveal a non-Legend card to the opponent durably. The eight cards that
print "reveal" are `fool-on-the-hill`, `hanako-arasaka-in-a-gilded-cage`,
`judy-alvarez-nothing-to-doubt`, `misty-olszewski-mender-of-broken-spirits`,
`sasha-yakovleva-wont-let-you-down`, `sketchy-ripper`, `viktor-vektor-sit-down-and-relax`, and
(negatively) `kiroshi-optics`. AUD-misty-…-1 above is filed as Misty's instance; the fix is one
engine affordance, not eight scripts.

**U-2 · N-NOTE — MaxTac Suppression Team vs. a printed "can attack the turn it's played".**
`legal.attack_permission` (legal.py:33-40) returns `(False, False)` for any lagged Unit whose Rival
controls a suppressor **before** the Unit's own `attack_perm` hook runs (legal.py:44-47). So MaxTac
Suppression Team beats Nadia's "this Unit can attack their Gig area the turn it's played", beats
ADRENALINE and beats GO SOLO. `docs/rules.md` states no "can't beats can" precedence rule and no
ruling covers it, so both readings are arguable — but this is a *settled repo decision*, not an
accident: the code comment says "full stop", and `tests/cards/test_statics.py::
test_maxtac_suppression_stops_adrenaline` pins the ADRENALINE case. I confirmed Nadia
(`attacks == set()`) and a GO SOLO `v-corporate-exile` (`attack_permission == (False, False)`) both
follow the same rule, i.e. the engine is self-consistent. No test filed: writing one would freeze
whichever reading I picked, and I have no authority to pick.

**U-3 · S-SUSPICION — cost-reduction floor vs. a cost *increase*.**
All three reducers floor inside `self_cost` (`max(1, base - n)`) and `ops.play_cost` floors again at
ops.py:358-359 only when the *net* delta is negative. With enough reducers plus an increaser the two
orders could differ (e.g. base 7, −8 from Octant, +2 → engine 3; "floor last" → 1). I wrote the
probe and it cannot be reached: the only two `cost_mod` hooks in the pool are
`viktor-vektor-drop-your-illusions` (wnc.py:817-821, CYBERWARE Gear only, negative) and `riot-shield`
(wnc.py:831, `+2` and only when `go_solo` is true, while `self_cost` is skipped for GO SOLO at
ops.py:348). Nothing can raise the cost of a Unit played from hand, so no legal game distinguishes
the two orders. Filed as a note, not a finding.

## Clean

Each of these was walked clause by clause against `data/cards/wnc.json`, and each behaviour below
was confirmed by a probe I ran against the live engine before deleting the probe file.

* **`corpo-security`** — "This Unit can't attack. / BLOCKER". wnc.py:741 `extra={"cant_attack": True}`
  → `legal.py:29-31`, an unconditional early return that no `attack_units_now` / `attack_ready_units`
  mod can override. BLOCKER comes from the JSON `keywords`, not the script, and still works:
  probed a ready Corpo Security redirecting a Gig-area attack (`Block` offered). Nothing in the
  script the text does not say.
* **`ruthless-lowlife`** — "This Unit can only attack rival Units. (It can't attack Gig areas.)"
  wnc.py:766 `attack_perm=lambda c, b: (b[0], False)`: the Unit leg is passed through untouched
  (so Lag and ADRENALINE still govern it) and only the Gig leg is forced off — exactly the
  parenthetical. Probed: no `Attack` offered against a Gig-only board; the attack resolves normally
  against a spent rival Unit.
* **`valentino-guerrera`** — "If you have more ★ (Street Cred) than a Rival, this Unit can attack
  ready Units with BLOCKER." wnc.py:771 `extra={"attack_ready_blockers_if_more_cred": True}` →
  `legal.py:56-62`: `s.street_cred(p) > s.street_cred(r)` (strict "more", checked live per attack,
  read off the attacker's own script) AND `has_keyword(s, u, Keyword.BLOCKER)` (so a *granted*
  BLOCKER counts too). Probed all three corners: with more cred the ready BLOCKER is a target and a
  ready non-BLOCKER is not; at equal cred the ready BLOCKER is not a target. Ruling 038 (Null Street
  Cred "smaller than 0 when compared") is satisfied by the plain `>`: gig faces are clamped to
  `1..sides` in `ops.set_gig`/`ops.adjust_gig`, so `street_cred == 0` ⟺ no Gigs ⟺ Null, and no board
  can tell Null from a real 0.
* **`mtod12-flathead`** — "If you have less ★ (Street Cred) than a Rival, this Unit can't be
  blocked." wnc.py:776 `unblockable=lambda c: c.less_cred()` → `legal.py:183-185`, which drops every
  `Block` option from the defender's reaction menu while leaving Calls and QUICK Programs alone
  (correct: the text forbids blocking, not reacting). Unqualified in the text and unqualified in the
  code — it applies to Unit attacks as well as Gig-area attacks. Probed behind (no `Block`), level
  (`Block` offered) and ahead (`Block` offered). Same ruling-038 note as Valentino.
* **`maxtac-suppression-team`** — "Rival Units can't attack the turn they're played." wnc.py:781
  `extra={"suppress_new_units": True}` → `registry._hook_row` slot 7 → `ops._rebuild_active` slot 10
  → `legal.py:33-40`. "the turn they're played" is `s.i_lag[unit]`, which `engine.play_card` /
  `engine.go_solo` set on entry and `EndTurnCleanupStep` clears for every Unit each turn
  (ruling 039), so it is exactly "entered the field this turn". "Rival" is `1 - s.i_owner[unit]`
  indexed against the flag of the *other* seat — probed that the controller's own freshly played
  ADRENALINE Unit still attacks, and that the Rival's does not. Control never changes hands in this
  set (no card prints "gain/take control"), so owner == controller. See U-2 for the precedence note.
* **`jacked-in-voodoo-boy`** — "This Unit can't attack unless you played a Program this turn."
  wnc.py:786: `b` when `c.played_this_turn(lambda i: c.is_type(i, PROGRAM))` is non-empty, else
  `(False, False)`. `s.played` is appended by `engine.play_card` (engine.py:180) and cleared by
  `EndTurnCleanupStep` (steps.py:182), so the window is exactly "this turn"; the owner filter in
  `effects.played_this_turn` is safe because no card moves a card between owners. Passing `b`
  through unchanged is right: satisfying the condition removes the card's own restriction, it does
  not exempt the Unit from Lag. Probed both branches.
* **`nadia-fighting-through-grief`** — "If a Rival controls more Gigs than you, this Unit can attack
  their Gig area the turn it's played." wnc.py:791-792 `(b[0], True) if c.s.i_lag[c.inst] and
  len(c.gigs(c.rival)) > len(c.gigs()) else b`. Three details all check out: the count is **Gigs**
  (dice), not Street Cred; only the Gig leg is turned on, so she still cannot attack Units the turn
  she lands; and outside the played-turn the hook returns `b`, adding nothing. Probed: 1 vs 2 Gigs →
  her only legal target is the Gig area and the steal resolves; 2 vs 2 → no attack at all; 0 vs 1 →
  she attacks and steals.
* **`octant`** — "Play this Unit for -1 €$ for each friendly Gig with 8+ value, to a minimum of
  1 €$." wnc.py:797 `max(1, base - gigs_8plus(c))`; `dsl.gigs_8plus` (dsl.py:44-45) counts
  `v >= 8` over `c.gig_values(player)` — the die **face**, inclusive at 8, over the controller's own
  Gig area (stolen dice sitting there are friendly, correctly). Probed the boundary
  (faces 7/8/12 → 7−2 = 5), zero Gigs (7) and the floor (eight 20s → 1).
* **`maxtac-heavy`** — "Play this Unit for -1 €$ for each of a Rival's Units, to a minimum of
  1 €$." wnc.py:802 `max(1, base - len(c.units(1 - p)))`, keyed off the `player` argument
  `ops.play_cost` passes in. `state.units` counts field Units only: attached Gear is excluded, and
  Legends still in the Legends area are excluded while a GO SOLO Legend on the field is included —
  which is right, since GO SOLO plays it "as a ready Unit". Probed 2 rival Units behind a Gear and a
  face-up Legend (5) and 7 rival Units (floor 1).
* **`trauma-team-operatives`** — "Play this Unit for -1 €$ for each Unit in your trash, to a minimum
  of 1 €$." wnc.py:807 `max(1, base - sum(1 for i in c.trash(p) if c.is_type(i, UNIT)))` — **your**
  trash (probed: a Rival's trash full of Units changes nothing), Units only (a Program and a Gear in
  the same trash are not counted), floor 1 at eight Units.

`corpo-security` and `misty-olszewski-mender-of-broken-spirits` are also clean in the script → text
direction for clause 1: `extra={"cant_attack": True}` is read in exactly one place and does nothing
else. No card in this batch carries a hook the printed text does not ask for.
