**Headline:** Kerry Eurodyne's printed "If you control a Gig with 8+ value" is a **resolution**
condition, but the script also hard-codes it as an **activation** restriction (`legal=`), so a line
the card allows — spend Kerry, draw nothing, collect Alt Cunningham's "when a friendly equipped Unit
is spent, draw 1" — is not offered at all.

**On Rogue Amendiares.** This audit independently reached the same defect batch B01 filed as
`AUD-rogue-amendiares-queen-of-the-afterlife-1` (steal trigger compares against `c.power(unit)` out
of situation). It is real, it reproduces from a second, unrelated board, and it is **not** re-filed
as a test here — a finding id belongs to one test, and `tests/cards/test_audit_shape.py` enforces
that. It is written up below for the record, with the independent reproduction, and this batch adds
the boundary control B01 lacks.

## Findings

### AUD-kerry-eurodyne-the-last-rockerboy-1 — B4 (scope/condition error: a resolution condition used as an activation restriction) — NEW

**Card:** `kerry-eurodyne-the-last-rockerboy` (MS01-WNC 12, Red Unit, cost 4, power 5, ROCKER /
SAMURAI, no printed keywords).

**Printed text**
```
⊡: If you control a Gig with 8+ value, draw 2.
```

**The clause:** the whole line — specifically *where* the "If …" sits relative to the colon.

#### Leg B — clause map

| # | Clause | wnc.py | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "⊡" — spend this card; the cost line carries no €$ | 1463 `self_spend=True`; `cost` left at its default `0` | ok |
| 2 | Lag stops ⊡ (rules.md 120/186, ruling 014) | not in the script — `legal.ability_options` (legal.py 94-98) skips a self-spend ability on a spent or lagged Unit | ok |
| 3 | "If you control a Gig with 8+ value" **as part of the effect** | 1463 `c.draw(2) if gigs_8plus(c) else None`; `dsl.gigs_8plus` reads `c.gig_values(None)` → the ability's own controller, and tests `v >= 8` (so "8+" includes 8) | ok |
| 4 | "draw 2" | 1463 `c.draw(2)` | ok |
| — | *extra, not in the printed text*: 1464 `legal=lambda c: gigs_8plus(c) > 0` | an added activation restriction | **the defect** |

The printed condition is inside the effect, after the colon, not beside the cost.
`docs/effects-authoring.md` describes `Ability.legal` as an "extra precondition"; everywhere else in
this file it guards something the ability *needs in order to function*, never a printed `If`:

| wnc.py | `legal=` | what it guards |
|---|---|---|
| 657 | `c.is_active_turn() and can_call_free(...)` | Call-a-Legend, already once per turn by ruling 041 |
| 1135 | `bool(c.hand())` | the discard is part of the **cost** |
| 1279 | `bool(c.gigs()) and bool(c.gigs(c.rival))` | a mandatory two-sided Gig swap |
| 1298 | `bool(c.gear()) and any(not c.equipped(u) ...)` | a mandatory Gear move |
| 1343 | `bool(c.min_gigs())` | a mandatory min-Gig read |
| **1464** | **`gigs_8plus(c) > 0`** | **restates a printed `If`** |

The decisive comparison is the sibling card with the identical printed shape.
`judy-alvarez-braindance-maestro` (wnc.py 1328-1338) prints
"⊡: Trash the top card of your deck. If it's a Program, you may add it to your hand." and its
`Ability` carries **no** `legal` — it may be spent with an empty deck and does nothing. Same for
`dum-dum-maelstrom-triggerman`'s "+1 power for each of its equipped Gear" at zero Gear. Kerry is the
only card in the set whose script converts its own printed `If` into a legality test.

Spending a Unit is not a null act in this game, which is what makes the restriction a lost line of
play rather than a cosmetic difference:

* `alt-cunningham-mother-of-daemons` — "When a friendly equipped Unit or Legend is spent, draw 1"
  (wnc.py 1005-1008). Spending an equipped Kerry draws a card whatever the Gigs show. That is the
  test below.
* `rita-wheeler-no-stupid-questions` — "The first time this Unit is spent each turn, draw 1, then
  discard 1" — the set clearly cares about the act of spending, independently of why.
* Spending also changes the board: `legal.attack_targets` (legal.py 61-63) offers only **spent**
  rival Units as fight targets, so choosing to spend is a real tactical decision with a real cost
  (ruling 024 makes the same point about attackers).

`docs/rules.md` states exactly one restriction on using a ⊡ effect — Lag (lines 120 and 186) — and
no rule anywhere that an activated effect may be used only when its conditional payload would do
something.

**Rulings checked.** 014 (a lagged Unit may not activate ⊡ — correctly enforced in `legal.py`, not
here), 019 (once-per-turn scope — Kerry has no once-per-turn clause, so the Uncertain trap does not
bite), 021 (Sell is main-phase only — unrelated), 024 (an attacker is spent and can be attacked
later), 036 and 041 (the two "only once even if an effect says so" limits, neither of which is a
plain ⊡). None of the six PRESUMED-D areas (BLOCKER redirect scope, GO SOLO slot vacation,
once-per-turn scope, field size, squares-on-Gear, payment order) is touched by this finding.

**Controls (pass today):**
* `test_braindance_judy_may_be_spent_with_nothing_to_find` — the same printed shape on a sibling
  card *is* offered with nothing to find, and resolves to nothing.
* `test_kerry_draws_two_with_an_eight_plus_gig_and_only_counts_your_own` — the payload is right: an
  8 on your Gigs draws 2, and a rival's 9 does not count. So the defect is the gate and nothing else.

**Honest counter-reading.** A reader who holds that an activated ability may not be used when its
payload cannot resolve would defend the gate, and that is a real position in some games. Two things
push against it here, and are why this is filed as **B4** rather than **A**: (a) the payload *can*
resolve — it resolves to drawing zero cards, which is not the same as being impossible; and (b) this
file's own house style for the identical printed shape is the opposite, on a card printed in the
same set. The fix is one line: delete `legal=…` and keep the `if gigs_8plus(c)` already in the
effect.

### AUD-rogue-amendiares-queen-of-the-afterlife-1 — B4 (scope/condition error: which power) — **already filed by batch B01; reproduced here, not re-filed**

**Card:** `rogue-amendiares-queen-of-the-afterlife` (MS01-WNC 126, Blue Unit, cost 5, power 4,
keyword QUICK).

**Printed text**
```
The first time another friendly Unit steals a Gig with value less than its power each turn, ready 2 Eddies.
QUICK 2 €$, ⊡: A rival Unit loses power equal to this Unit's power this turn.
```

**The clause:** "…steals a Gig with value **less than its power**".

#### Leg B — clause map (all twelve clauses, both abilities)

| # | Clause | wnc.py | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "The first time … each turn" | 1471 `c.once("ready_eddies")`, placed **last** in the `and` chain so a non-qualifying steal does not burn it; `EndTurnCleanupStep` (steps.py 183) clears `s.once` for both seats every turn | ok |
| 2 | "another" | 1470 `e[1] != c.inst` | ok |
| 3 | "friendly" | 1470 `c.s.i_owner[e[1]] == c.player` | ok |
| 4 | "Unit" | implicit — only a Unit (or a GO SOLO Legend, a Unit on the field) can be `e[1]` of a `steal` event (`steps.do_steal`) | ok |
| 5 | "steals a Gig" | 1470 `e[0] == "steal"`; `steps.push_steals` emits one event per die, so a multi-steal gets several chances at clause 1 | ok |
| 6 | "with value less than its power" | 1471 `e[4] < c.power(e[1])` — `e[4]` is the stolen die's value ✓, but `EffectCtx.power` defaults to `sit=0` | **wrong power** |
| 7 | "ready 2 Eddies" | 1472 `c.ready_eddies(2)` → `ops.ready_eddies(s, c.player, 2)`, which touches only `Zone.EDDIES` | ok |
| 8 | "QUICK … ⊡" (second ability) | 1477 `Ability(..., self_spend=True, quick=True)`; offered in the reaction window on the rival's turn | ok |
| 9 | "2 €$" | 1477 `cost=2` | ok |
| 10 | "A rival Unit" | 1476 `temp_power_one(c, c.rival_units(), -p)` — `optional=False`, so exactly one, mandatory, from the rival's side as seen from Rogue's controller | ok |
| 11 | "loses power equal to this Unit's power" | 1475 `p = c.power()` — Rogue is not attacking, so `sit=0` is the right reading for *her*; buffs on her count; ruling 029 floors the victim at 0 inside `ops.power` | ok |
| 12 | "this turn" | `ops.add_temp_power` appends to `s.temp_power`, cleared by `EndTurnCleanupStep` (steps.py 179); `ops.py` 213-214 also drops a card's entries when it leaves play (CR 5.3.2.2) | ok |

Clause 6 is the defect. "Its power" binds to the friendly Unit that is stealing — a Gig has no
power, and Rogue calls herself "this Unit" one line later — and at the instant of a steal that Unit
is **attacking**. The engine reads exactly that power everywhere else in the same attack:

* `steps.stealable` (steps.py 268) — `pw = power(s, thief_unit, ATTACKING)`, which is also where the
  one other printed value-vs-power comparison in the set (Chrome Fang) is applied;
* `steps.ResolveAttackStep` (steps.py 333) — `n = steal_count(power(s, a, ATTACKING))`.

The house style for a script reading an attacker's own power agrees: `swordwise-huscle` uses
`c.power(sit=ATTACKING) >= 5` inside `on_attack`. Rogue's hook is the one place asking for the plain
number, so a Unit standing next to Saul Bright *Stormrider* ("Other friendly Units have +2 power
while attacking") steals a die the engine agreed it could steal at power 8, and Rogue then judges
that same steal against power 6.

**Rulings checked.** 005 (stolen dice keep their face, so `e[4]` is the value the card means),
009 (an empty Gig area is not a legal target), 011/012 (BLOCKER redirect — Uncertain, and untouched:
a redirected attack never reaches the Gig-steal branch), 019 (once-per-turn scope — Uncertain, and
the script sits **on** its default, so the trap does not apply and clause 1 is not a finding),
024, 029 (power floors at 0 — clause 11 only). None licenses reading an attacking Unit's power
without the attack.

## Unproven

**U-UNREACHABLE-1 — Judy's `type is not LEGEND` guard (wnc.py 1454).** A Legend on top of the deck
would be denied the free play, which ruling 033 otherwise permits for any costed Legend ("Any Legend
with a cost (CR 4.5); in this set every costed Legend carries GO SOLO"). But no legal game puts a
Legend in a deck: Legends start in the Legends area, and ruling 034 removes one from the game the
moment it leaves the field or the Legends area ("trash, hand or deck are invalid areas for a Legend
(CR 4.4.1)"). `board()` can plant one there by hand, but the resulting position is not reachable, so
the guard is unfalsifiable and is in fact defensive. The companion guard `c.d(i).cost is not None`
(same line) is dead for the same reason — a scan of `data/cards/wnc.json` finds **zero** non-Legend
cards with a null cost. Bucket: **U-UNREACHABLE**.

**U-UNREACHABLE-2 — Rogue's drain measures her power before the target question is answered.**
`drain` computes `p = c.power()` (wnc.py 1475) and closes over the integer, then `temp_power_one`
queues the "which rival Unit" question. If anything could change Rogue's power between the question
and the answer, the delta would be stale. Nothing can: the choice is the next step on the stack, and
`engine.activate` (engine.py 221-229) pushes the effect's `HookStep` *before* paying and
self-spending, so even the ⊡ cost cannot land in that window — and spending does not change power
anyway. There is no legal game state inside the gap. Bucket: **U-UNREACHABLE**. (Measuring at the
start of the effect's own resolution is also the ordinary reading of "equal to this Unit's power";
`test_rogue_drain_counts_her_own_temporary_power` pins that it *does* include buffs standing at that
moment.)

**N-NOTE-1 — "Reveal" is not modelled as a reveal to the Rival (engine-wide, not a card defect).**
Judy *Nothing to Doubt* prints "**Reveal** the top card of your deck"; the script moves it straight
to hand (wnc.py 1453, with a comment saying so). `core.view.knows_identity` treats `Zone.HAND` as
readable only by its owner, and `s.i_known` is consulted **only** for the `LEGENDS` zone
(view.py 121-137) — `ops.call_legend` (ops.py 547) is its single writer of `0b11`. So no engine
mechanism exists for making a hand card public to the other seat, and every "reveal …, add it to
your hand" card in the set has the same gap (`viktor-vektor-sit-down-and-relax` via `search_top`,
`sketchy-ripper`). Batch A14 records the same observation from the other side. Filing it against Judy
would be filing an engine limitation as a card bug, and it is in any case not expressible in the
assertion vocabulary this batch is allowed to use (zones, hand size, `power()`, `has_keyword`).
Bucket: **N-NOTE**.

## Clean

* **`judy-alvarez-nothing-to-doubt`** (wnc.py 1446-1458) — "1 €$, ⊡: Reveal the top card of your
  deck. You may play it for free. Otherwise, add it to your hand." Clause by clause: `cost=1` plus
  `self_spend=True` (1458) ✓; `c.top(1)` with an empty-deck early return (1449-1451) ✓; "you **may**"
  is `c.maybe` with a real decline branch (1457) ✓; "play it **for free**" is `play_free` →
  `play_card(..., cost=0)`, which for a Gear asks for a host and for a Program routes through
  `Zone.LIMBO` per ruling 035 ✓; "otherwise, add it to your hand" holds because the card is put in
  hand first and only leaves it if the free play is taken (1453) — the net zone is identical either
  way ✓; and the continuation uses the ctx it is given (`free(c2)`, 1455-1456), as
  `docs/effects-authoring.md` requires ✓. Controls
  `test_judy_nothing_to_doubt_equips_a_revealed_gear_for_free` and
  `test_judy_nothing_to_doubt_with_an_empty_deck_does_nothing` both pass. The only gaps found are the
  two Unproven entries above, neither of which is a defect in this script.
* **`kerry-eurodyne-the-last-rockerboy`** — clauses 1-4 all correct, including "8+" as `>= 8` and
  "you control" as the ability's own controller; the single defect is the extra `legal=` gate.
* **`rogue-amendiares-queen-of-the-afterlife`** — eleven of twelve clauses correct: the `once`
  bookkeeping and its short-circuit position, the "another / friendly" scoping, "ready 2 Eddies"
  touching only the Eddies area, and the entire second ability (QUICK reaction timing, 2 €$ + ⊡ cost,
  one mandatory rival Unit, her own buffs counted, floored at 0, expiring at end of turn). The single
  defect is the `sit` argument in clause 6, already filed by batch B01.
