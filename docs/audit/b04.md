# Batch B04 — activated abilities: costs, the spend symbol, legality guards and QUICK

Cross-cutting pass. I read one mechanism across the whole pool rather than one region of the file,
so the unit of evidence here is a **table of every activated ability in the set** and the sentence
"N of them do X; this one does Y, and its printed text does not say Y".

**Costs, the spend symbol and QUICK are 19/19 faithful.** Every printed €$ number matches `cost=`;
every ability that prints `⊡` sets `self_spend=True` and the one that prints no `⊡` (Panam
*Strength Through Family*, "During your turn, you may Call a Legend for free") sets it `False`;
the five that print QUICK set `quick=True` and the fourteen that do not set it `False`. The card
`keywords` list agrees with the ability text on all five QUICK cards, and the seven QUICK Programs
are handled by `reaction_menu` off the same keyword. Johnny's variable cost is the only callable
and it is the only card that prints a variable one. That axis is clean; the defects are all in
`legal=`.

## The majority pattern, and the two shapes that break it

13 of the 19 abilities carry **no `legal=` at all**, and nine of those thirteen are, on some legal
board, an ability that can do nothing — and they are all still offered. Measured against the real
registry (`tests/cards/audit/test_b04.py` builds these boards):

* Goro with no friendly Units, Dum Dum with no friendly Units — **offered**.
* Evelyn with no rival Units — **offered**.
* River Ward with no Gear in hand — **offered**.
* Padre, Wakako, Muamar, Dexter with **no dice anywhere on the board** — all **offered**.
* Judy *Braindance Maestro* with an empty deck — **offered** (A15's control).
* Alt Cunningham's **second** ability with an empty trash — **offered**.

So the pool's settled convention, and the engine's own default, is: *you may pay the cost and spend
the card; the effect then finds nothing and does nothing.* Nothing in `docs/rules.md` contains a
"no legal target, no activation" rule, and `ability_options` implements none — `legal=` is the only
way a card gets one, and it is opt-in per card.

Against that, the six `legal=` guards fall into three kinds:

1. **A printed restriction** (correct). Panam *Strength Through Family*: "**During your turn**, you
   may Call a Legend for free" → `is_active_turn()`, plus `can_call_free` for the printed
   once-per-turn parenthetical. This is the only guard that re-states words on the card.
2. **A printed *condition of the effect*, promoted to a condition of activation** (wrong).
   Kerry *The Last Rockerboy*, "⊡: **If you control a Gig with 8+ value**, draw 2" → already filed
   as `AUD-kerry-eurodyne-the-last-rockerboy-1` by the A15 section pass. **Alt Cunningham
   *Soulkiller Architect* ability #0 is the same shape and was missed.** That pairing is the whole
   point of a cross-cutting pass: one section reader saw Kerry, another read Alt three hundred
   lines away and had no Kerry to compare her with.
3. **A "the target does not exist" guard** (wrong, but a softer claim). Hanako, Panam *Nomad
   Cavalry*, Overwatch *Panam's Gift* — where nine siblings with exactly the same problem have no
   guard at all.

### FINDING 1 — `AUD-alt-cunningham-soulkiller-architect-1`

> "⊡: Your next Program this turn plays for -1 €$ for each friendly min Gig, to a minimum of 1 €$."

With no min Gig the discount is **-0**: the effect is fully defined, it simply subtracts nothing.
There is no target, no "if", and no condition printed anywhere near the cost. `legal=lambda c:
bool(c.min_gigs())` nonetheless removes the ability from the menu, so **Alt cannot be spent at
all** on such a board.

That is not a cosmetic difference. Spending is itself a payoff in this set: Netwatch Netdriver
("When this Unit or Legend is spent, draw 1"), Arasaka Emergency Radioport and Tetratronic Rippler
all equip to a face-up Legend and pay out when the host is spent. Equip a Netdriver to Alt, show no
min Gig, and the printed line "⊡: …" is a draw the engine refuses to let you take. The control
beside the finding is the same Legend's **other** ability, which with an empty trash is exactly as
useless and is offered without complaint.

The "to a minimum of 1 €$" floor itself is *correct*, and handled centrally in `ops.play_cost`
(`if delta < 0 and base >= 1: total = max(1, total)`); I checked it against every card in the set
that prints a reduction (Carnage, Octant, MaxTac Heavy, Trauma Team Operatives, Zetatech Berserk,
Viktor *Drop Your Illusions*, Nocturne) and all of them print the same floor. Not a finding.

### FINDING 2 — `AUD-hanako-arasaka-daughter-of-the-emperor-1`

> "⊡: Swap a friendly Gig with a rival Gig."

Guarded by `bool(c.gigs()) and bool(c.gigs(c.rival))`. Padre *Man of the Cross* prints the
identically shaped "⊡: Set a player's Gig to the same value as another player's Gig" — it also
needs a die on each side of the table — and carries no guard: with **zero dice on the whole board**
Padre is offered, is spent, and does nothing. Two Legends, the same printed shape, opposite
treatment. The majority (and the engine default) is Padre's; Hanako is the odd one out, and the
consequence is the same as Alt's — a Netdriver on Hanako is a draw you cannot take while the rival
Gig area happens to be empty.

I did **not** file separate findings for Panam *Nomad Cavalry* (`gear() and an unequipped Unit`) or
Overwatch *Panam's Gift* (`bool(hand())`): the argument is word-for-word Hanako's, the fix is one
policy decision rather than three, and a reviewer who settles Hanako settles all three. They are
listed here so the list is complete. If the project instead decides that "no legal target ⇒ no
activation" is the house rule, then the fix runs the other way and nine abilities need guards
added; either way the pool should not hold both answers at once.

## Checked and correct (no finding)

* **Lag.** CR 11.3.1 / `docs/rules.md` glossary: "Units with Lag can't attack or activate
  self-spend effects." `legal.ability_options` blocks a lagged Unit's ability **only when
  `ab.self_spend`** — Judy *Nothing to Doubt* cannot use her `⊡` the turn she is played, while
  Panam *Strength Through Family*, whose ability prints no `⊡`, can. The engine agrees with the
  rule, including the part of the rule that is a non-restriction. Confirmed in
  `tests/cards/audit/test_confirmed_b04.py`.
* **A Legend may not spend itself toward its own cost** (ruling 027). `ability_options` and
  `engine.activate` both pass `exclude=inst` for a self-spend ability on a Legend, and they agree:
  face-up Goro with 0 Eddies cannot activate his `QUICK 1 €$, ⊡`, with 1 Eddie he can. Confirmed.
* **QUICK is owner-relative.** `_ctx` sets `ctx.player = s.i_owner[inst]`, not `s.active`, so
  "a **rival** Unit loses power" activated by the *defender* during the attacker's turn correctly
  reads the attacker's side. Rogue's reaction drops a power-4 attacker to 0 and it steals nothing
  (rules table: 0 power steals 0 Gigs). This is real coverage the pool did not have — every other
  QUICK test in the repo runs on the controller's own turn. Confirmed.
* **QUICK inside the reaction window composes.** Goro's QUICK grants BLOCKER mid-attack and the
  window reopens with `Block(that Unit)` newly legal, then his own "When a friendly Unit uses
  BLOCKER" trigger fires. Confirmed.
* **`self_spend` on Gear spends the Gear** (ruling 030, Class D): `engine.activate` calls
  `spend(s, inst)` on the ability's own instance and `ability_options` reads that instance's spent
  flag, so Overwatch spends itself and its host keeps its ready state. Presumed deliberate, and the
  code matches the ruling.
* **Cost is paid before the effect and is not refunded when the effect fizzles** — correct; a cost
  is not conditional on the effect doing something.
* **Panam *Nomad Cavalry*'s "from this Legend"** — `c.gear()` with no argument is `gear_on(self)`,
  i.e. the Gear on Panam, not all friendly Gear. Correct.
* **The five batch cards with no activated ability** — Viktor *You Might Feel a Little Pinch*
  (PLAY), Saburo (static), Royce (static + GO SOLO), V *Streetkid* (CALL + GO SOLO) and
  V *Corporate Exile* (no script at all, its text being pure GO SOLO reminder) — carry no
  `Ability`, and should not. The brief's "18 cards carry abilities" is 13 in fact.
* **"Reveal the top card of your deck"** (Judy *Nothing to Doubt*) does not make the card known to
  the rival once it reaches hand. This is **not** a card-script defect: `view.knows_identity` has
  no mechanism for a known card in a hidden hand (`i_known` is Legend-only, `_pinned` only ever
  shows the *chooser* their own cards), and all eight cards in the set that print "Reveal … add to
  your hand" are equally unmodelled. It is an engine-level gap, out of scope for a card audit, and
  filing it against Judy alone would mislabel it.
