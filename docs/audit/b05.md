# Batch B05 — cross-cutting pass: replacement & permission effects
### `CardScript.attack_perm`, `CardScript.self_cost`, `CardScript.unblockable`, and the `script.extra` dict

One mechanism, whole pool. The unit of evidence here is **the complete population of each hook**
— not a region of `wnc.py` — and the sentence "N of them do X, this one does Y, and its printed
text does not say Y". Authority order: `data/cards/wnc.json` `text` → `docs/rules.md` →
`docs/rulings.md`.

Assigned cards (16): nadia-fighting-through-grief, sandayu-oda-hanakos-guardian, mtod12-flathead,
maxtac-heavy, octant, adrenaline-converter, satori-sword-of-saburo, the-relic-experimental-biochip,
royce-dont-call-me-simon, towerfall, pyramid-song, gunpoint-diplomacy, reboot-optics,
safety-override, chrome-reverie, maxtac-suppression-team. All 16 read end to end, plus every other
member of the four hook populations (carnage-at-the-colosseum, nocturne-op55n1,
we-gotta-live-together, trauma-team-operatives, zetatech-berserk, ruthless-lowlife,
jacked-in-voodoo-boy, corpo-security, misty-olszewski-mender-of-broken-spirits, valentino-guerrera,
johnny-silverhand-never-stop-fighting), and every engine site that reads one.

**Headline:** the permission family is the one family in this set where the engine is *consistently*
right about the hard part. Every "can't" in it is a claim about every path, and every path in the
engine funnels through one function: `legal.attack_permission` for attacking, `legal.reaction_menu`
for blocking, `ops.play_cost` for cost. There is no second door. The four `attack_perm` scripts each
grant exactly the half of `(units_ok, gigs_ok)` their text names and no more; all seven `self_cost`
scripts carry the floor their text prints, and that floor is printed on **every** cost reduction in
the set, which is what makes `ops.play_cost`'s blanket `max(1, …)` correct rather than lucky; and
every one of the four `extra` keys has a live read site, with no key written and unread and no key
read and unwritten.

## 1. `attack_perm` — the whole population is four scripts

`attack_perm(ctx, (units_ok, gigs_ok)) -> pair`, consulted **last** in
`legal.attack_permission` (`src/cptcg/core/legal.py:44`). Because it is last it can widen as well as
narrow, so the check that matters is *which half* and *under what condition*.

| card | printed | returns | half granted / denied | gated on Lag? | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nadia-fighting-through-grief | "If a Rival controls more Gigs than you, this Unit can attack **their Gig area** the turn it's played." | `(b[0], True)` | grants the **Gig** half only | yes, **and** rival-Gigs > mine | correct |
| sandayu-oda-hanakos-guardian | "This Unit can attack **rival Units** the turn it's played." | `(True, b[1])` | grants the **Unit** half only | yes | correct |
| ruthless-lowlife | "This Unit can only attack rival Units. (It can't attack Gig areas.)" | `(b[0], False)` | denies the **Gig** half | no — unconditional, as printed | correct |
| jacked-in-voodoo-boy | "This Unit can't attack unless you played a Program this turn." | `b` or `(False, False)` | denies **both** | no — gated on `played_this_turn(PROGRAM)` | correct |

The table is the finding I did not get to write. Two of the four widen and two narrow, and each one
touches exactly one component of the pair: Nadia never gains the right to attack a Unit, Sandayu
never gains the right to attack the Gig area, and Ruthless Lowlife's `b[0]` leaves the Unit half
exactly where the lag branch left it. Measured on the real registry (confirmations 1 and 2):
Nadia on arrival is offered the Gig area and *not* the rival's spent Unit; Sandayu on arrival is
offered the rival's spent Unit and *not* the Gig area. That pair of tests is what the
already-existing pin in `tests/rules/test_pool_rules.py` cannot say — it only asks whether the Unit
can attack *at all*.

Two further things a per-card reader cannot see:

* **Only the attacker's own script is consulted.** `attack_permission` reads
  `s.reg.defs[s.i_card[unit]].script`, so `attack_perm` is a self-permission by construction and
  there is no way for one card to hand another its `attack_perm`. The pool's two "give a Unit
  haste" cards (Yorinobu *Steel Dragon*, Johnny *Rocking Renegade*) therefore correctly use the
  `attack_units_now` mod instead, which is read inside the lag branch.
* **Prohibition beats permission, and the ordering in `attack_permission` is what enforces it.**
  The `cant_attack` mod (Chrome Reverie, Nocturne), the `cant_attack` extra (Corpo Security,
  Misty Olszewski) and MaxTac Suppression Team's `suppress_new_units` all `return (False, False)`
  **before** `attack_perm` is ever called. So MaxTac stops Nadia and Sandayu, not only ADRENALINE —
  which is the property confirmation 3 pins, and which B02's confirmation (ADRENALINE only) does
  not reach. A card whose text prints a permission and a card whose text prints a prohibition meet
  here, and the prohibition wins every time.

### A pool-wide sweep, not a reading

For every Unit in the registry I placed it unlagged on a board where the rival holds a spent Unit
and two Gigs, and recorded `attack_permission`. The only Units that are not `(True, True)` are
Corpo Security `(False, False)`, Misty Olszewski `(False, False)`, Jacked-in Voodoo Boy
`(False, False)` and Ruthless Lowlife `(True, False)` — exactly the four whose text prints a
restriction, and no others. No Unit restricts attacking without printing it, and no Unit that
prints a restriction fails to carry one.

## 2. `self_cost` — the whole population is seven scripts

`self_cost(ctx, player, base) -> cost`, applied in `ops.play_cost`
(`src/cptcg/core/ops.py:348`) before every `cost_mod` delta and skipped for GO SOLO (no Legend in
the set has one, so that branch is unreachable, not wrong).

| card | printed | code | floor | conditional as printed? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| octant | "-1 €$ for each friendly Gig with 8+ value, **to a minimum of 1 €$**" | `max(1, base - gigs_8plus(c))` | 1 | n/a — per-Gig |
| carnage-at-the-colosseum | same sentence, Program | `max(1, base - gigs_8plus(c))` | 1 | n/a |
| maxtac-heavy | "-1 €$ for each of a Rival's Units, to a minimum of 1 €$" | `max(1, base - len(c.units(1 - p)))` | 1 | n/a |
| trauma-team-operatives | "-1 €$ for each Unit in your trash, to a minimum of 1 €$" | `max(1, base - #UNIT in trash(p))` | 1 | n/a |
| zetatech-berserk | "-1 €$ for each friendly face-up Legend, to a minimum of 1 €$" | `max(1, base - len(legends(p, faceup=True)))` | 1 | n/a |
| nocturne-op55n1 | "If your fixer area is empty, play this Program for 1 €$." | `1 if c.fixer_empty() else base` | n/a (a set price) | yes |
| we-gotta-live-together | "If a Rival controls at least 2 more Gigs than you, play this Program for 3 €$." | `3 if len(gigs(rival)) >= len(gigs()) + 2 else base` | n/a (a set price) | yes |

Five scale, two set a price, seven of seven carry the floor or price their text prints. Measured:
MaxTac Heavy is 5 €$ against two rival Units (printed 7), **1 €$** against eight, and 6 €$ against
one; Octant is 5 €$ with a d20 showing 8 and a d10 showing 10 in the Gig area and a d6 showing 6
that does not count. Playing MaxTac Heavy with exactly five Eddies leaves zero (confirmation 6), so
the discount is honoured at payment and not only in the menu.

**The cross-cutting fact about the floor.** `ops.play_cost` applies a *blanket* `max(1, …)` to any
negative delta, with the comment "every printed reduction says 'to a minimum of 1 €$'". I checked
that claim against the whole pool: the reductions are Octant, Carnage, MaxTac Heavy, Trauma Team
Operatives, Zetatech Berserk, Viktor Vektor *Drop Your Illusions*, Alt Cunningham
*Soulkiller Architect* and Nocturne's GO SOLO clause — **eight of eight print "to a minimum of
1 €$"**. Johnny *Rocking Renegade*'s ability discount is the one reduction that prints no minimum,
and it is the one that is written `max(0, 2 - gigs_8plus(c))` rather than `max(1, …)`. The comment
is a true statement about the card pool, not an assumption, and a ninth card without the phrase
would silently acquire a minimum it does not print.

**Street Cred is never Null in a way these cards can see.** Four cards in this batch branch on
"more ★ / less ★ than a Rival" (Royce, MTOD12 Flathead, Towerfall, Gunpoint Diplomacy) and
`EffectCtx.more_cred` / `less_cred` compare raw sums, while ruling 038 makes an empty Gig area
*Null* — "smaller than 0 when compared", and `EffectCtx.cred_even` special-cases it. That looks like
a gap and is not one: a die never shows a face below 1, so a non-Null Street Cred is always ≥ 1 and
Null-as-0 orders identically to Null-as-below-0 on every reachable board. Only `cred_even` can tell
the difference (0 is even, Null is neither), which is exactly the helper that handles it.

## 3. `unblockable` — the whole population is one script

`unblockable(ctx) -> bool`, read once, in `legal.reaction_menu`
(`src/cptcg/core/legal.py:184`). MTOD12 Flathead — "If you have less ★ (Street Cred) than a Rival,
this Unit can't be blocked" — is the only card in the set with the hook, and the only card in the
set that prints "can't be blocked". A card with no sibling is where a defect hides, so the
prohibition was checked as a claim about every path: **blocking in this game exists only as the
BLOCKER redirect** (`Block` is offered nowhere else; the only other text containing "redirect" is
Secondhand Bombus's BLOCKER reminder), and the `unblockable` test gates that whole branch —
including the ruling-012 case where a BLOCKER redirects an attack aimed at a *Unit* rather than a
Gig area, which the `atk.target_kind == TARGET_GIG or cfg.blocker_redirects_unit_attacks` clause
sits behind. Confirmation 4 pins both target kinds and both sides of the Street Cred test.

## 4. `script.extra` — four keys, five cards, four live read sites

| key | written by | read at | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cant_attack` | corpo-security, misty-olszewski-mender-of-broken-spirits | `legal.attack_permission:30` | read; returns `(False, False)` before `attack_perm`, so an unqualified "can't attack" beats every permission in the set |
| `suppress_new_units` | maxtac-suppression-team | `registry._hook_row:177,180` → `ops._rebuild_active` slot 10 → `legal.attack_permission:38` | read; keyed to the **opponent** of the lagged Unit, and only inside the Lag branch (ruling 039: Lag *is* "played this turn"; ruling 027: a GO SOLO Legend enters lagged, so it is caught too) |
| `attack_ready_blockers_if_more_cred` | valentino-guerrera | `legal.attack_targets:59` | read; the ready Unit must actually have BLOCKER (`has_keyword`, so a granted BLOCKER counts) and the ★ test is the attacker's own |
| `wins_vs_tag` | johnny-silverhand-never-stop-fighting | `steps.fight:360` | read; see below |

No key is written and unread; no key is read and unwritten. (Contrast `must_attack`, written twice
and read nowhere — already `AUD-mox-inciters-1` — and `attack_gigs_now`, read once and written
nowhere: those are the two halves of the mod family that do not join up, and the `extra` family has
neither.)

One thing worth writing down, because it is the mirror image of five open findings. `wins_vs_tag`
implements "This Unit wins all fights against **CORPO Units**" as `tag in s.card(y).tags` with *no*
test that `y` is a `CardType.UNIT`. Five other scripts in the set add exactly that type test and
five findings have been filed against them for it (`AUD-satori-sword-of-saburo-1`,
`AUD-6th-street-recruits-1`, `AUD-river-ward-…-1`, `AUD-saburo-arasaka-…-1`,
`AUD-jackie-welles-mamas-favorite-1`) because ruling 015 makes a GO SOLO Legend on the field a Unit.
`wins_vs_tag` is the member of the family that gets it right, and it gets it right for a reason that
holds generally: the only cards that can ever be in a fight are the ones `legal.attack_targets` and
`legal.reaction_menu` offer, and both offer `s.units(player)`, which includes a GO SOLO Legend on the
field and excludes everything else. The type test is not merely unnecessary there — it is wrong
everywhere it appears.

`steps.fight` also shows the engine's settled precedence, which is the same precedence
`attack_permission` uses: `wins_vs_tag` (a permission) is applied first, then `no_defeat_in_fight`,
then the 0-power rule, then `next_fight_no_defeat` — prohibitions last, so they win. The single
departure is `next_loss_defeats_winner` (Safety Override), which is applied after all of them and
sets a defeat back on; that is recorded in §6.

## 5. The six assigned cards that already carry an open finding

Named so this batch is not read as having missed them, and because each was re-derived here from the
mechanism rather than taken on trust:

* `AUD-gunpoint-diplomacy-1` (A02) — "the next time this Unit attacks this turn" granted as a
  turn-long `attack_ready_units` mod. The rest of the card is right: the *Unit* is chosen by the
  caster and only the *effect* choice moves to the rival on less ★, matching "they instead choose
  one effect for you", and `both=True` resolves the two clauses in printed order.
* `AUD-reboot-optics-1` (A03) — the shield is consumed only by a fight it actually saves a Unit
  from.
* `AUD-safety-override-1` (B04/B02) — "loses a fight" skips a tie.
* `AUD-the-relic-experimental-biochip-1` (A08, **fixed while this batch was running**) — "Then,
  bottom-deck this Unit" was skipped when the trash held no eligible Unit. The `another` exclusion is right: `was_host` is written by
  `ops.defeat` for each Gear on the defeated host and `hosts[-1]` is the current one.
* `AUD-satori-sword-of-saburo-1` (A12) — the loser is tested with `CardDef.type is UNIT`.
* `AUD-memory-relapse-2` / mod-family items touching Chrome Reverie, Octant, Towerfall,
  Pyramid Song and MaxTac Suppression Team were settled by B02 and re-checked here against the
  permission mechanism rather than the mod mechanism; nothing new.

## 6. Examined and not filed

**6.1 Safety Override is applied after every prohibition in `steps.fight`.** `next_loss_defeats_winner`
sets `defeat_a` / `defeat_t` back to `True` after `no_defeat_in_fight`, the 0-power rule and
`next_fight_no_defeat` have cleared them — so Safety Override kills through Muamar Reyes's
"can't be defeated in a fight" and, in the `t_wins` case, through my own Reboot Optics (whose
`if defeat_t and …` guard never even runs, so the shield is not consumed either). It is the one
place in the engine where a permission is resolved after a prohibition. B02 §4.2 examined this and
declined to file on the grounds that "defeat the opposing rival Unit" reads as an *effect*
defeating the Unit rather than the fight doing it, and Reboot Optics' wording ("**it** doesn't
defeat the opposing friendly Unit") is, if anything, a weaker claim still. Recorded, not filed —
and named here so the third agent to find it does not file it either.

**6.2 Adrenaline Converter grants ADRENALINE to a face-up Legend host.** `kw_mod` returns true for
`inst == c.host()` with no test that the host is a Unit, and the card prints "this **Unit** has
ADRENALINE" over "(Equip to a friendly Unit **or face-up Legend**.)". I could not construct a board
where it is observable: `has_keyword(…, ADRENALINE)` is read in exactly one place,
`legal.attack_permission`, which is only ever called for a card on the field, and a Legend that
reaches the field did so via GO SOLO, which already carries its own attack permission. An
unobservable difference is not a printed-text finding.

**6.3 `play_cost` floors the sum of the deltas, not each one in turn.** Riot Shield's "+2 €$ to use
GO SOLO" and Nocturne's "-2 €$ … to a minimum of 1 €$" are added together before the floor is
applied, so a base cost of 2 would come out 2 where clause-at-a-time gives 3. The cheapest GO SOLO Legend in
the set costs 5, so `base - 2 >= 1` always and the floor never binds; and ordering questions like
this are settled deliberately by ruling 025. Not a finding.

## 7. Clean — read against the printed text and found correct

* **nadia-fighting-through-grief / sandayu-oda-hanakos-guardian** — the half each grants, the Lag
  gate, Nadia's Gig-count condition (counts, not values, and re-read on every call), and the fact
  that a rival MaxTac Suppression Team beats both. Confirmations 1–3.
* **mtod12-flathead** — the only `unblockable` in the set; both target kinds, both sides of the ★
  test, and no second blocking path to leak through. Confirmation 4.
* **adrenaline-converter** — the only `kw_mod` in the set. The condition is the pool's standard
  "at least 2 more Gigs" comparison (identical to We Gotta Live Together's), it is re-read on every
  `has_keyword` call rather than granted once, and the ADRENALINE it produces is enough to beat Lag.
  Confirmation 5.
* **maxtac-heavy / octant** — discount, floor and payment, all three measured. "A Rival's Units"
  resolves through `GameState.units`, which includes a rival GO SOLO Legend on the field and
  excludes Gear, which is ruling 015's answer. Confirmation 6.
* **valentino-guerrera** — the one `extra` key nobody had exercised: a ready **BLOCKER** becomes a
  legal target while ahead on ★, a ready non-BLOCKER never does, and neither does the BLOCKER while
  behind. Confirmation 7.
* **royce-dont-call-me-simon** — the "instead" widens the threshold from 2 to 3 and nothing else;
  the defeat is mandatory (no `optional=True`), and with equal ★ a power-3 rival Unit survives while
  with more ★ it does not.
* **towerfall / pyramid-song** — "choose both instead" resolves the clauses in printed order via
  `later`, the bottom-deck candidates are recomputed after the power drop, and ruling 029's floor at
  0 is what makes the "power 0" clause reachable. Pyramid Song's inlined `k == 4 and v == 1` is
  exactly `min_gigs()` restricted to a d4 ("Indices of dice showing their minimum face (1)").
* **chrome-reverie** — the prohibition is a mod read by `attack_permission`, so it is honoured on
  every attack path, and `until_my_next_turn` on your own turn stores `turn + 1`, which
  `EndTurnCleanupStep` keeps through the whole of the rival's turn.
* **maxtac-suppression-team** — the flag is keyed to the lagged Unit's opponent, applies only while
  the Unit has Lag, and beats ADRENALINE, GO SOLO, the `attack_units_now` grants and both printed
  attack-on-arrival permissions.
* **gunpoint-diplomacy, reboot-optics, safety-override, satori-sword-of-saburo,
  the-relic-experimental-biochip** — everything except the already-filed clause.
